### preparation-to-be-a-rock

- `preparation-to-be-a-rock`: running from
  a storm on the horizon to a readiness declared complete and set inside a
  network. The Elaborations run chronologically and the argument runs with
  them, which is not true of every thread here, so the section can be built in
  date order without further arrangement.
  Foundation, anchor: 2015-08-17-af-jesus, the premise at its most urgent.
  Great change is coming, "the storm is just upon the horizon," and the
  gathered must prepare themselves and prepare places of refuge, "those
  lifeboats of Light for those who are caught in the storms," so that they can
  be channels of Love through the chaos.
  Foundation section, beneath the anchor: 2015-06-29-af-confucius, the
  calm-before-the-storm call, redouble prayer, accelerate cleansing, release
  attachments, so that the storm finds the seeker clothed in the raiments of
  Light and strong enough to serve.
  Elaborations. 2016-05-12-af-alec-gaunt gives the readying a purpose the
  Foundation does not state, readiness to be sent, "pick up at a moment's
  notice and be guided to a place, a person, an opportunity," with "even
  material encumbrances" among what is shed; 2016-08-11-af-alec-gaunt supplies
  the outward half of a process otherwise described from the inside, the
  world's resistance as proving ground, "the error, the hurtful allegations,
  the difficulties, the complexities that are placed before you," with the
  turn "And now the time has come"; 2016-09-16-af-john-the-beloved relocates
  the footing and states the safety claim outright, "Anything that comes will
  bypass you and you will be safe. You will stand upon the rock of God's great
  Will and lead others in this Truth," with the condition attached, that to
  understand God's Will the soul must be fully awakened by the Love;
  2016-12-07-af-andrew gives the rock argument plainly, "For in any storm the
  soul that is calm, the soul that steadies those around it is the one that
  will be looked to"; 2016-12-08-af-augustine gives the readying through the
  soul's own perceptions sharpened by the angels, "you need these perceptions
  of what I speak, you need to be able to know the will of God, to be able to
  perceive the support and protection and guidance that the angels will give
  you to navigate through a world that will have many difficulties and
  upheavals," the glimpse being of possibilities and of what to do in the
  moment, "It will be clear and obvious what you must do in any given moment";
  2016-12-21-af-alec-gaunt paces the readying to the changes, "as these coming
  storms approach, so God accelerates your growth, opens your gifts, prepares
  you for what is to come," names what is readied as riches already laid in
  the soul, "This has purpose. This will be called upon," and the cost of the
  use, "this will not often be in a way that is easy or gratifying to you,"
  with the rock in an image of its own, "you must remain the pillar of
  strength, the bearer of truth, the loving channel of God," as the way to
  care for the loved ones the seeker fears for;
  2017-04-20-af-augustine declares the readiness, "essentially you have come
  to a place where you are ready," the opening to serve tied to the collapse
  of what mankind has built, "As this fabric created by mankind becomes
  shredded and collapses, the door will open for you to serve";
  2017-06-06-af-seretta-kem makes all they have lived through the readying,
  "All of your experiences, all of these teachings, all of our messages, all
  of God's Blessings, this great unfolding of light in your life are a
  preparation," gives the rock in so many words, "You must be the rock, my
  beloveds, the rock of comfort, of truth, of love," and tells them not to
  picture what is coming, "Do not let your imaginations give you ideas as to
  what this will be like," since what carries them is "a surety and a faith"
  already within, the seeker's side of what 2019-01-28-af-augustine says from
  the spirit side;
  2017-06-23-af-seretta-kem sets the rock against the sand, "much of it has
  been built upon sand that will wash away soon. In these preparations you
  begin to build a new foundation upon the rock of Truth," a foundation built
  "within yourself," with the prepared moving "in this world but not of this
  world";
  2017-09-13-af-keea-atta-kem makes the readying God's own
  work, "this is God orchestrating your life, preparing you for what is to
  come," and sends the prepared to scientists, leaders and seekers;
  2018-09-01-af-seretta-kem marks the readiness turn, years of preparation
  near fruition and the readying completed through mind-soul alignment,
  "strong with your feet firmly planted upon the ground and your soul
  connected with Heaven," moving the argument from change is coming to you are
  now ready; 2018-10-24-af-seretta-kem widens it to a collective architecture,
  the prepared souls "one component of a vast network that is being built" and
  coordinated by God and angels, with the caution that "the future is not
  written in stone" so readiness is developed day by day;
  2019-03-13-af-stephen gives the same mechanism in another form,
  dreams, portents, insights and visions as God communicating through
  the soul in advance, "so that when that time to act comes, all is not
  confusing and foreign and misunderstood."
  Objection removed. 2019-01-28-af-augustine answers the demand the readying
  keeps provoking, which is for specific predictions. The refusal is honest
  rather than evasive: conditions are erratic, the veil distorts, and spirit
  experiences time differently, so "we cannot steer the ship for you... you
  have within you a compass." The preparation is therefore inner rather than
  informational, which clears the expectation that being a rock depends on
  knowing the future.

  readying strand of an earth-changes message, carried with the fence;
  2016-12-28-af-alec-gaunt, "for you will be a rock. You will be steady in the
  storms to come," with awareness of the world asked for alongside the gaze
  on God; 2017-01-15-af-peter, "You must prepare within yourselves," with the
  circle building "an oasis of light"; 2017-07-09-af-matthew, "God has
  invested a great deal of light within you to prepare you for these moments
  to come"; 2017-08-01-af-augustine, the strength asked of those who will lead
  others through the upheavals, "You must recognize the strength of your
  souls"; 2017-08-05-hm-jesus, the steady soul others turn to, "Stride

  for later need.
  Structural note: the fence is that a message must carry the readying itself,
  God's preparation of a soul for what is coming, and not merely the coming
  changes or a general call to grow. It is not that the message must contain a
  rock, a refuge, or someone leaning: under the argument-not-image ruling a
  thread is defined by its argument rather than its image, so messages that
  carry the readying without the image stand as witnesses. The slug keeps its
  image and is therefore narrower than the thread, which is accepted rather than
  overlooked. 2016-09-16-af-john-the-beloved inverts the image outright, since
  the soul stands upon a rock which is God's Will rather than being one, and
  it is a member on the strength of its argument, that the thread's stability
  is contingent on a prior soul awakening.
  A second thing: if a Capstone is wanted, 2018-09-01-af-seretta-kem is the
  obvious candidate, since a readiness declared complete is a close. Weigh it
  against leaving the thread open, which may be truer to material that keeps
  saying the time is near.
  Fences. Against `vanguards-of-change`, the registry fence: rock is stability
  for what comes to you, vanguard is mobility for where you are sent.
  2016-05-12-af-alec-gaunt holds a role in both, on the same release, so the
  fence has to be stated rather than assumed. Against `earth-changes`: that
  thread is what the changes are and why they come, this one is the readying
  of the people who will meet them. The log makes the split at
  2017-09-13-af-keea-atta-kem, where the earth-changes reading was called the
  primary home, and at 2016-10-15-af-seretta-kem.
