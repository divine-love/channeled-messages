### earth-changes

- `earth-changes`: running from why the Earth is changing to what the changes
  are for and what they ask of a prepared soul.
  Foundation, presumptive anchor: 2015-08-17-af-jesus, a developed statement
  of the premise, the cleansing of the Earth, environmental abuse, and a
  coming time of chaos, "the storm is just upon the horizon." The Earth "has
  been abused so greatly by the will of men... and the Earth is fighting
  back," the agent being "God's Creation, which responds to the actions of
  men," and the end a cleansing followed by "a thousand years of peace upon
  this world."
  Elaborations, what the changes are and what causes them.
  2016-12-21-af-alec-gaunt gives the cause as scale and law together: the
  world "at a breaking point, as the populations of your world put more and
  more stress upon this beautiful planet," with the response built into
  Creation, "there are safeguards in place in the laws of God's creation which
  prevent the unravelling of the natural world," and the scale named as what
  changed, "When the world had a small population such actions were absorbed
  without any great detriment to the planet." The changes come "so that you
  may adjust and prepare with each onslaught," and what follows is renewed
  rather than undone, "many of your technologies and accomplishments will
  remain but how they are used and utilized will change";
  2017-09-13-af-keea-atta-kem gives the manifestation material, magnetic
  shifts, animal disruption, and the closed-system consequences of ignoring
  the Laws of Creation, and sends those carrying Divine Love to scientists,
  leaders and seekers; 2018-08-13-af-seretta-kem gives the intensifying
  imbalance and humanity's blindness to its causes, with the claim that true
  solutions will not come from more inventions or structures but from each
  soul turning honestly inward and seeking God.
  Elaborations, what the changes are for. 2016-10-15-af-seretta-kem makes the
  purpose the Earth's own readying, "There will be indeed many storms and
  shakings of the Earth, risings of the tide, as the Earth cleanses and
  readies itself for a new era of life upon your Earth," with the cause stated
  almost as the registry has it, "it is the disharmony which mankind has
  brought within themselves and within this world that has sparked these
  changes and responses... you cannot, my beloveds, ignore the Laws of
  Creation, you cannot be immune to the world that responds to your
  indifference"; it carries a second facet not the ground of its role, the
  awakened soul's stance given its content as adaptability and hope, "be
  flexible, willing to change and adapt as all around you changes," "you
  cannot carry fear but hope."
  2023-02-10-af-augustine makes the purpose the waking of humanity, the Earth
  itself changing to force mankind awake, which moves the thread from what the
  changes are to what they are for. The two purposes differ in whose they are,
  the planet's own era against our awakening.
  Elaboration, what the changes do to people. 2016-09-06-af-mary names the
  effect as the reason prepared souls are wanted, "Mankind is on the verge of
  great change as this world is transformed, as God places His Hand upon this
  planet to bring change and healing and balance. And he desires that his
  emissaries of Love will help ease the fears and the confusion that will
  ensue from these changes." Explicit that the Hand is God's and the intended
  end is balance, as the Foundation also has it.
  Objection removed. 2017-06-06-af-seretta-kem clears the belief that the
  suffering the changes bring is sent by God, "God is not cruel, my beloveds.
  God does not bring suffering to others or to you. It is humanity that
  brings suffering upon itself by not following the Laws of Creation." The
  changes are the Laws answering what people have done, "reactions and
  consequences brought about by the Laws of Creation," which gives the law
  reading that the structural note weighs for 2016-12-21-af-alec-gaunt and
  2017-01-15-af-peter.
  Witnesses. 2016-12-05-af-augustine, the cause and the remedy together,
  humanity having "stressed many places upon this world to the point of
  breaking" and God having "set in motion a change agent that will bring about
  a balancing of the world, a healing of this planet"; 2016-12-07-af-andrew,
  the world and the soul changing together, "The world is changing and you are
  changing," with the Earth to be "healed and brought back into balance";
  2017-01-15-af-peter, the cause as humanity's collective will and its
  "desire for the material above all else," the remedy placed outside human
  effort, "these dilemmas, beloved souls, cannot be cured by the will of man";
  2017-07-17-af-augustine, the fires outside the window as "a manifestation of
  a lack of Love," with the change beginning in the seeker's "own inner world
  and thus the world around you".
  Fences. Against `preparation-to-be-a-rock`: that thread is the readying of
  souls; this is the Earth's own changes and their purpose. Against
  `earth-conditions-and-prayer`: that thread is the man-made darkness-field
  acting on the individual soul; this is the planet. An adjacency holds no
  role here: 2019-01-28-af-augustine on the limits of specific prophecy, whose
  primary home is `preparation-to-be-a-rock`.
  Structural note: the registry says the changes are a cleansing, not a
  punishment, and 2016-12-21-af-alec-gaunt words them close to one, "a harsh
  lesson is coming to the world," with "consequences for those who are
  resolutely blind and deaf to the truth of Creation." The ground it gives is
  law, "with each choice comes consequence," and it calls the outcome "a
  blessing," so the two fit if the lesson is the working of the law rather
  than a sentence. 2017-01-15-af-peter has the same shape, "Now humanity will
  reap the consequences," and adds "Humanity is not evil," which leans toward
  the consequence reading. Read together at build.
