### divine-love-without-knowing

- `divine-love-without-knowing`: running from what the mind does not need to
  know, through what the soul receives anyway, to where the seed opens later.
  Foundation, anchor: 2019-01-31-af-augustine, an answer to the thread's own
  question, "why and how a soul may receive the Divine Love without the
  conscious understanding of its mechanisms and protocols to receive this
  gift." Understanding helps but is not required, "It does not require that
  the mind has the specific understanding of these mechanisms and laws...
  Though it is helpful," because "The soul on its own accord can long for God
  and receive this gift as long as the mind does not obstruct this intention
  expressed by the soul." Other beliefs do not bar it, many already hold some
  of the Love "often not to a great degree," and the seed may open later, "the
  seed has been planted and the experience lingers within the soul and
  therefore a door may be opened in the future," often in the world of spirit.
  Elaborations. 2016-05-25-af-alec-gaunt turns the claim into a rule of
  conduct, reading the soul rather than the stated belief, "a soul that is
  receptive to God's Love, yet in their minds has a different definition of
  what this is, is still a soul on the Divine Path nonetheless";
  2016-09-02-af-augustine explains why the Love goes unrecognized, not through
  ignorance of doctrine but because it is hard to tell apart, "it is difficult
  to delineate between the mind, the natural love, and soul experiencing the
  Divine Love," so the unknowing is misattribution rather than absence;
  2016-11-27-af-augustine has the recognition happen between people rather
  than within one, those without the teaching "nonetheless... in the light and
  know the light, and are drawn to the light," with "an acknowledgement of
  this common spiritual light and love" on both sides;
  2019-02-05-af-keea-atta-kem moves from not knowing to not feeling, "For
  those who do not feel the inflowing of the Divine Love within their souls,
  this does not mean that they are not receiving this Love, for indeed they
  are," with the promise of "some powerful experience, some form of awakening"
  in time.
  Witnesses. 2016-10-29-af-andrew separates being embraced from knowing it,
  the embrace "always present, always there," while "to be embraced by God in
  a conscious way is to know your own soul," so knowing changes the experience
  rather than causing the embrace; 2016-12-01-af-tahlia, the seed that opens
  later, often in spirit, planted by those who speak the truth, "a seed is
  planted, something has changed, a shift, an opening"; 2016-12-02-af-jesus,
  the hunger named in a prayer, "those who hunger and know not what they
  hunger for"; 2017-05-11-af-jesus, the thirst that does not know itself,
  "They don't even know their own thirst, they don't know where to turn."
  Fences. Against `mind-vs-soul`: that thread asks which faculty perceives
  truth; this one asks whether the soul can receive while the mind does not
  understand. The Foundation serves both. Against `redemption-universal`: that
  thread is reception after refusing the Love; this one is reception without
  knowing it. Against `two-paths`: that thread is a choice made knowingly.
  Structural note: 2016-09-02-af-augustine, from the same spirit, asks the
  Foundation's question from the other side and could stand in the Foundation
  section beside it; weigh at build. The thread does not argue against
  teaching: the Foundation itself says understanding "does lead more quickly
  to being receptive," and 2016-04-02-af-jesus gives the reason to teach,
  "first they must know and understand what it is that they yearn for."
