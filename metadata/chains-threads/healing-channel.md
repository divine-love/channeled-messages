### healing-channel

- `healing-channel`: running from where healing comes from, through what makes
  a person a clear channel for it, to what a few such channels do.
  Foundation, anchor: 2016-01-04-af-seretta-kem, given to a group formed to
  bring healing to the world. Healing comes from God through the channel, "the
  most powerful way is through sincere prayer allowing God's beautiful healing
  to flow through you and through this group"; the intention "starts with the
  soul, my beloveds, it does not start with the mind"; mental constructs lower
  the group and draw lesser influences by the Law of Attraction; singleness of
  purpose keeps the channel clear; and a few such souls change the world, "It
  does not take many souls to be in this beautiful condition."
  Elaborations, what makes the channel clear. 2015-03-27-af-andrew makes being
  healed within the precondition, "In order to be a clear channel of Love one
  must be healed within to allow the love of God to do its work";
  2016-02-10-af-confucius gives the working method, "pray for God to touch
  that soul through you," with the precondition, "Remember to nourish your
  soul foremost"; 2017-06-08-af-augustine gives the reason for healing
  oneself first, "you cannot teach others if you have not followed the same
  path and are a little further ahead upon it," and "If you are not strong
  within yourself, you cannot be strong for another," with the healing left
  to God, "God will do the work."
  Elaborations, where the channel reaches. 2016-05-02-af-mary carries it into
  ordinary daily care, "whether it be your children, or the elderly or those
  in deep need, ask your Heavenly Father to make within you a channel of
  Love," with the result exceeding what natural love achieves;
  2016-07-25-af-seretta-kem shows the work leaving the circle, "this one great
  beam of Light is multiplied over and over again exponentially and touches
  many, many thousands in the world."
  Witnesses. 2015-06-15-af-joseph-smith, the calling without institutional
  standing, "this does not require labels or positions within any church";
  2016-01-08-af-jesus, "a great channel of service for mankind," in a
  commissioning message; 2016-09-03-af-mary, channels of "love, acceptance,
  humility and prayer" raising the conditions of a gathering;
  2016-09-14-af-augustine, the maturity the channel requires, "grow to a sort
  of maturity where you can control these conditions within you in order to be
  a channel of the highest magnitude"; 2016-10-17-af-keea-atta-kem, the method
  reduced to closeness to God, "By merely being close to God and in being
  close to God you are attuned to the flow of His Love in the world."
  Fences. Against `divine-love-healing`: that thread is what Divine Love
  healing is and how it compares with other healing; this one is how a person
  becomes a channel for it. Against `bell-toll-instrumentality`: that thread
  is a signal that goes out whether or not anyone is in view; this one is a
  deliberate conduit for a blessing directed to someone. Against
  `vanguards-of-change`: that thread is the commission and the freedom it
  needs; 2016-01-08-af-jesus is its Foundation and only a witness here.
  Against `prayer-changes-world-conditions`: 2016-07-25-af-seretta-kem is that
  thread's Foundation, where the route of prayer to distant souls is the
  subject; here it shows only what a clear channel's work becomes.
  Structural note: 2016-11-26-af-andrew, on being a channel of Love whose
  outcome is ignited longing, is set aside here because it has no healing
  language.
