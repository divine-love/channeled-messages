### guidance-as-seed

- `guidance-as-seed`: running from how God's Will announces itself to what
  the soul owes the announcement.
  Foundation, anchor: 2016-02-02-af-jesus. God's Will comes "as a seed of a
  thought, a small inkling within you and it grows as His Love nurtures this
  seed," and the soul is to "listen, to grasp, to honor" it and "act upon this
  in loving faith." The whole argument stands in this one message, which is
  why it anchors on its own.
  Elaborations. 2016-12-12-af-augustine gives why guidance comes unclear and
  what the soul does then, in a sun image where the Foundation has a seed: the
  Will "obscured by the clouds of the human condition," with clearings that
  bring "inspiration, even revelation," then reworked by the mind, "Whatever
  God has guided you to do often gets distorted by your minds as you think and
  review this gift of inspiration." The seeker returns to prayer, "ask for
  confirmation of this guidance," and guidance acted on that turns out poorly
  is not failure, "You are guilty of nothing other than trying."
  2016-12-28-af-alec-gaunt fits the form of the guidance to each soul's
  gifts, "For some, it may be a thought that arises in your mind, a feeling to
  do something," and "for others, you may have detailed visions, powerful
  understandings, strong impressions," with the honour step and its reason,
  "Be aware that God is trying to get through to your conscious self."
  Witnesses. 2016-09-02-af-confucius carries the instruction to act, "Listen
  to the desire of your soul. Listen to that truth that beats within you, each
  one of you, and be compelled to act upon that which is within you," in a
  different image, a spark and a flame where the Foundation has a seed and a
  droplet; under the argument-not-image ruling the change of image does not
  disqualify it. 2016-10-19-af-goldie names the promptings and their source,
  "God has a plan for each soul who is willing to listen to their guidance,
  their promptings, and their inspirations. They come from God and His
  angels," without saying how they arrive, how they grow, or what honouring
  them requires; the willingness-to-listen clause is the nearest it comes to
  the honour-and-act step. 2017-01-02-af-seretta-kem names the quiet form and
  asks that it be followed, "Come to know the guidance, the promptings, those
  subtle whispers which will guide you in life." 2017-04-10-af-james has the
  Will placed in the soul first, "The image is produced first," and the
  honour step as desire, "It is the deep desire to know God's Will, allowing
  the iris to open." 2017-08-05-hm-jesus has the seeker ask God in prayer
  what He wishes, and the answer rise from within, "your soul will bring the
  answers forward," to be acted on, "the convictions of your soul knowing will
  influence your being, will influence your actions and your decisions."
  2017-08-06-af-jesus-2 asks the circle to act on what the soul knows, "Always
  listen to your souls, my beloveds," and "Do not hesitate when you know the
  steps you must take and the road you must follow."
  Fences. Against `divine-love-mediumship`: that thread concerns how a message
  crosses from spirit into a mortal mind; this concerns how God's own guidance
  surfaces inside a soul. 2016-07-04-af-jesus was checked against this thread
  and is not a match on that boundary, guidance there arriving through a human
  instrument rather than inwardly. Against `earth-conditions-and-prayer`:
  that thread is the field pressing on every soul; this one is the guidance
  the field obscures. 2016-12-12-af-augustine is an Elaboration here and a
  witness there.
  What is awaited. A message that develops the growth claim, that the inkling
  grows as Love nurtures it, rather than restating the prompting. The
  witnesses restate; none develops. 2016-12-12-af-augustine has growth on
  the receiving side instead, the soul clearing until the Will is plain, and
  2016-12-28-af-alec-gaunt has the faculties that receive it strengthened by
  use; both are a different claim.
  Structural note: the Foundation had no log entry; one is added at
  2016-02-02-af-jesus so the log and the roster agree. Separately, the
  Foundation has God's Will arrive as a seed of a thought, and
  2016-12-28-af-alec-gaunt keeps that for some souls while giving others
  "detailed visions." The two fit if the seed is the usual form rather than
  the only one. Read together at build.
