### simplicity-of-the-path

- `simplicity-of-the-path`: running from why the path is simple to why a soul
  stops complicating it.
  Foundation, presumptive anchor: 2016-04-17-af-francis-of-assisi, the path
  inherently simple and complication human-made, given as a developed statement
  with first-person Testimony. The scope covers both the walking and the
  speaking, the seeker's life and the servant's ministry, under the
  argument-not-image ruling.
  Elaborations. 2016-08-26-af-brother-mandus gives the claim as it applies to
  ministry, with a reason and a failure mode the Foundation does not give, "to
  espouse upon the values and understandings of esoteric religions falls at
  the feet of those who come for nurturance and comfort and healing. It has no
  effect; it is the power of love, the simplicity of a simple hello, I love
  you, that reaches the soul, that nurtures the heart," locating complication
  in what the servant says and saying why it fails; 2016-09-09-af-andrew
  argues the claim rather than asserting it and supplies a mechanism, that
  faith itself removes the appetite for analysis, "The world is a complex
  place filled with many complex ideas. The truth is not complicated; the
  truth is simple... when one is truly of faith there is no great desire to
  analyze, merely to act in accordance to the knowing of the soul." Where the
  Foundation and 2016-08-26-af-brother-mandus say the path is simple or show a
  ministry being simple, this says why a person stops complicating it;
  2016-12-02-af-augustine answers the complication of seeking more and more
  spiritual knowledge, "It is simple, it does not require great learning,
  research, building complex paradigms of understanding," since "it comes in
  your relationship with God, it comes in your sincere prayers to your
  Heavenly Father," keeps the simple register for ministry, "simple words,
  simple prayer, simple love is all that is required," and separates simple
  from easy, "It is very simple, my beloveds, but hard to do in a challenging
  world."
  Witnesses, the claim asserted. 2016-08-26-af-augustine, "It is a simple
  opening of yourselves to this blessing of Love... Powerfully, beautifully,
  simply"; 2016-08-28-af-faith-nyquist, the path-and-life assertion with a
  consequence attached, "it really is so very simple and as you live this
  simple life all the complexities will fall away or fall into place";
  2016-11-26-af-andrew, "It is so simple, my beloveds, so simple," with the
  manner named as innocence, simplicity and grace; 2016-12-03-af-matthew, the
  simplicity put in terms of time and effort, "it is simple, it takes little
  time, it takes but a sincere effort"; 2017-01-04-af-james, complex ideas
  "rendered into very simple understandings of the mind" as the soul grows;
  2017-01-25-af-alec-gaunt, "Make this simple prayer. Reach out to God in
  this simple way"; 2017-01-30-af-augustine, "It is simple," and "seek this
  Love in all its simplicity"; 2017-03-05-af-luke, "A simple act, a simple
  prayer, a willingness to be open... is all that is required";
  2018-11-28-af-augustine, "Yes, it is a
  simple path, is it not? It is not adorned with many complex ideas and this
  is for a reason."
  Witnesses, where the complication is located. 2016-09-04-af-augustine
  forbids argument itself rather than complexity, "Do not argue with others.
  Be sure of the Love. Do not express contrary opinions, but be sure in the
  love"; 2016-09-07-af-confucius puts it inside the seeker's own head, "Do not
  clutter your minds with speculation and doubt and fear," naming its three
  components as speculation, doubt and fear; 2016-11-27-af-james-reid puts it
  in the seeker's accumulated spiritual information, judged from the far side
  of death, "You need to shake off the clutter and the error and the
  irrelevant details and focus, focus, my friends, on love," which
  2016-12-02-af-augustine develops; 2016-11-28-af-goldie puts it in the
  seeker's own heaviness and makes joy the thing that simplifies, "Oh, how you
  wish to complicate life when it is so simple and joyful. Go with God and go
  with joy and life will be simpler, easier, lighter."
  Witness, the motive of complication. 2016-09-10-af-augustine gives
  complication a motive, "So many wish to complicate the truth, to control it,
  to have great power, the power to disseminate God's Will to mankind. This is
  such folly and error," and runs the simplification inward, "your lives
  become simple, your motivations, and goals, and ambitions, become simple."
  Both are stated rather than built.
  Sightings, kept for the boundary they mark. 2016-10-17-af-keea-atta-kem reads
  the same sentence from the other side, simplicity as the whole of the method;
  2016-11-27-af-augustine uses the word in its ordinary sense, "you, my
  beloveds, must simply be in harmony with God," rather than as the thread's
  claim.
  Fences. Against `religious-error`: that thread is the error institutions
  accumulate; this one is the register in which truth actually reaches a soul.
  The two would meet at the ambition step if the complication-motive facet is
  ever promoted.
  What is awaited. A message that develops the complication-motive facet, the
  will to control as the reason truth gets complicated, which would promote
  2016-09-10-af-augustine and join this thread to `religious-error`.
  Structural note: 2016-09-10-af-augustine is a full doctrinal address whose
  arguments are each carried by members elsewhere, so it sits at witness in
  every thread it touches.
