### the-gift-and-the-channel

- `the-gift-and-the-channel`: running from the gift as a means of carrying
  the Truth, through the work that goes on with it or without it, to the
  condition of the people the gift waits on, and why it waits.
  Foundation, anchor: 2017-06-06-af-seretta-kem has both halves. The gift is
  not certain, but God's Will is, "not all is guaranteed but God's Will will
  manifest regardless of the intransigence of this world." And the gift waits
  on the people who would carry it, whose part is "to maintain a light about
  you and within you, to keep your thoughts at a high level, to be loving at
  all times," letting go of "those conditions within your soul which blur the
  clarity and light within." The gift comes out of their whole wish for it,
  "it is in this deep desire and acknowledgement that the power and the
  beauty of this gift will manifest."
  Elaborations, the work does not wait on the gift.
  2018-01-21-af-professor-salyards holds the gift worth seeking for the
  attention it draws to the Truth, "this gift will bring great interest and
  awareness and is very much worth pursuing," while the Truth goes out either
  way, "even if you are not successful with this gift, you will bring this
  Truth forth."
  Elaborations, what the gift waits on. 2017-05-25-af-seretta-kem spells out
  the preparation, "a certain physical stamina, chemical balances within the
  body, good health and a mind clear of negativity and judgments," and puts
  the gift below the Truth, "The gift of Direct Voice is but one gift";
  2018-07-22-af-seretta-kem puts the example before the gift, "For you cannot
  bring forth a gift, however remarkable it is, without truly being an
  example of the truth," with the old patterns in the way, "these old issues
  and conditions and patterns must indeed fall away."
  Testimony. 2017-06-12-af-alec-gaunt tells of his own earth-life attempt at
  Direct Voice and why it failed, "We were not able to bring those gifts
  through because as a group we lacked the soul development to allow this
  power to manifest as we so desired," and says what this circle has that his
  group lacked, "a great deal of Divine Love within your souls."
  Witnesses. 2017-06-05-af-stephen, the medium and those around him being
  made ready, "we are preparing this instrument and those who are associated
  with him for the gift of direct voice"; 2017-10-25-af-yogananda, the Truth
  carried "whether you are endowed with the gift of Direct Voice or not";
  2018-06-24-af-luke, "But first you must be the epitome of this Love."
  Fences. Against `divine-love-mediumship`: that thread is how a medium
  becomes a clear channel and why every message comes through a person; this
  one is the gifts as a means for the Truth and what they wait on. Against
  `bell-toll-instrumentality`: that thread is the Love going out from a soul
  without words; this one is a gift sought to draw attention to the Truth.
  2017-11-03-af-nikola-tesla marks the edge: knowledge held back until
  humanity "is able to utilize these gifts with wisdom" is the same rule for
  all of humanity, not a gift a circle prays for.
  What is awaited: a message that says why the gifts come second to the
  Truth, as 2017-06-12-af-alec-gaunt says why the gift waits on the people.
  Structural note: the circle prays for the gift at its Direct Voice sittings,
  so every Direct Voice message is read for this thread.
