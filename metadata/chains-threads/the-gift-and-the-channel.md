### the-gift-and-the-channel

- `the-gift-and-the-channel`: running from the gift as a means of carrying
  the Truth, through the work that goes on with it or without it, to the
  condition of the people the gift waits on, and why it waits.
  Foundation, anchor: 2017-06-06-af-seretta-kem has both halves. The gift is
  not certain, but God's Will is, "not all is guaranteed but God's Will will
  manifest regardless of the intransigence of this world." And the gift waits
  on the people who would carry it, whose part is "to maintain a light about
  you and within you, to keep your thoughts at a high level, to be loving at
  all times," letting go of "those conditions within your soul which blur the
  clarity and light within." The gift comes out of their whole wish for it,
  "it is in this deep desire and acknowledgement that the power and the
  beauty of this gift will manifest."
  Elaborations, the work does not wait on the gift.
  2018-01-21-af-professor-salyards holds the gift worth seeking for the
  attention it draws to the Truth, "this gift will bring great interest and
  awareness and is very much worth pursuing," while the Truth goes out either
  way, "even if you are not successful with this gift, you will bring this
  Truth forth."
  Elaborations, what the gift waits on. 2017-05-25-af-seretta-kem spells out
  the preparation, "a certain physical stamina, chemical balances within the
  body, good health and a mind clear of negativity and judgments," and puts
  the gift below the Truth, "The gift of Direct Voice is but one gift";
  2017-06-12-af-seretta-kem says why the gift asks so much: the carrier will
  meet "many challenges upon this road" and must be able "to shine this light
  over great distances" and "to not compromise," with the Love in them
  speeding the way, "the Divine Love in your souls will indeed accelerate the
  process"; 2017-06-23-af-seretta-kem puts the Love first and the wish for
  the gift second, "The greatest factor regarding the success of these
  ventures is the amount of Divine Love that is present within your souls,"
  and "Resistance will kill this project," and says what the gift is for: in
  a time of fear the spoken word will not be believed, and "A great
  credibility will be given through these manifestations";
  2017-07-09-af-matthew adds the love between the people who carry it: the
  wish to know every detail of the gift is not to become "a distraction from
  the greater purpose of prayer," and their bond, "one that requires trust and
  honesty, love and commitment," builds "a great capacity for we in spirit to
  work with you";
  2017-07-16-af-goldie has each member carry a piece of the gift, "a piece of
  a big circle and that piece must match all the other pieces," with the gift
  coming when the whole ring is ready, "When the vibration and energy of this
  beautiful ring of light is perfected, there will be the great blessing of
  Direct Voice";
  2017-08-06-af-jesus-2 has the gift wait on the people rather than the place:
  the conditions in the church were already strong enough, "if you were
  prepared as you will be in the future, this could have happened," and the
  circle's prayer is what will let it come, "It is the fervency of your
  prayers and desire for success for the Direct Voice that will allow me to
  materialize";
  2018-07-22-af-seretta-kem puts the example before the gift, "For you cannot
  bring forth a gift, however remarkable it is, without truly being an
  example of the truth," with the old patterns in the way, "these old issues
  and conditions and patterns must indeed fall away."
  Objection removed. 2017-08-06-af-seretta-kem: the gift waits on the
  circle's harmony and on how they watch their thoughts, "if we are to work
  together in this close and intimate way you must reach for a higher
  condition," but this does not mean becoming someone else or being perfect,
  "We are not suggesting that you change everything within you. You cannot
  change your personality and your inclinations to a great degree," only
  stopping a dark thought when it comes, "when you fall into negative
  thoughts, that you choose to halt this pattern and to interrupt it."
  Reframe. 2017-08-05-hm-jesus turns the gift from the end of the work into
  its start. The work is not done when the gift comes, "as the true work
  begins afterwards," and goes on "in bringing it to many, in demonstrating it
  to different corners of the world." Until then the spirits lean on the
  people, "we very much rely on your desire for this gift, on your prayers and
  your degree of harmony, your commitment and dedication to this."
  Testimony. 2017-06-12-af-alec-gaunt tells of his own earth-life attempt at
  Direct Voice and why it failed, "We were not able to bring those gifts
  through because as a group we lacked the soul development to allow this
  power to manifest as we so desired," and says what this circle has that his
  group lacked, "a great deal of Divine Love within your souls."
  Capstone. 2017-06-14-af-augustine says why the gift comes second to the
  Truth: the Direct Voice of earlier times came "by natural spirits and
  mortals," without the Love, and was "a natural phenomenon"; the Love
  "changes every gift," and without the soul's longing the circle's gift
  would be "merely a physical manifestation." The goal is the Truth, "Your
  goal is to bring the truth of God's Love to mankind," so the order is set,
  "Do not put the cart before the horse, beloveds."
  Witnesses. 2017-06-05-af-stephen, the medium and those around him being
  made ready, "we are preparing this instrument and those who are associated
  with him for the gift of direct voice"; 2017-06-24-af-confucius, the
  purpose waiting on the people, "You cannot fulfill a high spiritual purpose
  if you are not in a spiritual condition that will facilitate these
  expressions of love"; 2017-07-06-af-seretta-kem, the Love first, "Remember
  that prayer is the foundation for this effort"; 2017-07-22-af-seretta-kem,
  what each must declare being that they are with God rather than that they
  prepare for the gift, "I do not say that they must declare I am preparing
  for this gift"; 2017-08-02-hm-augustine, a daily routine of prayer and
  trust as part of "the preparation for Direct Voice"; 2017-10-25-af-yogananda,
  the Truth
  carried "whether you are endowed with the gift of Direct Voice or not";
  2018-06-24-af-luke, "But first you must be the epitome of this Love."
  Fences. Against `divine-love-mediumship`: that thread is how a medium
  becomes a clear channel and why every message comes through a person; this
  one is the gifts as a means for the Truth and what they wait on. Against
  `bell-toll-instrumentality`: that thread is the Love going out from a soul
  without words; this one is a gift sought to draw attention to the Truth.
  2017-11-03-af-nikola-tesla marks the edge: knowledge held back until
  humanity "is able to utilize these gifts with wisdom" is the same rule for
  all of humanity, not a gift a circle prays for.
  What is awaited: a message that shows the gift once it has come, and what
  the Love has made of it beside the natural phenomenon.
  Structural note: the circle prays for the gift at its Direct Voice sittings,
  so every Direct Voice message is read for this thread.
