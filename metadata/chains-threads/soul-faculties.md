### soul-faculties

- `soul-faculties`: running from the faculties the soul holds, through what
  opens and sustains them, to what they become.
  Foundation, anchor: 2019-02-01-af-josephus, a teaching given on request that
  names the faculties one by one and says they are understood only by being
  lived, "To truly understand the faculties of the soul, one must experience
  them in action." The greatest is "the emerging awareness of God"; then
  perceiving spirits, knowing Truth, "Truth in a pure form can only be
  understood by the soul," sensing God's Will, "somewhat like an antenna
  within you," and the channel of Love itself; and in layers present in
  everyone and refined by the Love, memory, healing, creativity, original
  thought, the capacity to love and "That moral compass within you." The
  mechanism is the chambers, each with "a purpose, a gift, a capacity of
  function that awakens with the gift of this colourful light," and each
  soul's expression is its own, "You cannot compare your journey with
  another's."
  Elaborations, how the faculties come into use. 2016-05-16-af-keea-atta-kem
  gives the order, the gifts lying "dormant without the Love's influence,"
  then the soul influencing "your thinking and doing and being" as the
  encrustation falls away, and the limit on trusting what is seen, "even in
  this you see but a reflection of the truth"; 2016-12-28-af-alec-gaunt
  has use itself strengthen them, "as you exercise these faculties of the soul
  they become stronger and clearer," with the Love as "the great activator
  within your soul"; 2017-04-10-af-james takes one faculty apart, the
  expression of God's Will, as a camera working in reverse, the image of Truth
  placed in the soul by the Love, "the iris is your will and your desire,"
  "The lens is your mind," clear when all three align and otherwise "leading
  to much interpretation and guesswork"; 2018-06-18-af-matthew has the
  faculties held open as well as opened, "It is only the Divine Love of God
  that not only awakens this new way, but sustains and develops and brings it
  into fruition"; 2018-10-09-af-seretta-kem brings them into line with
  consciousness as the two minds merge, "Your capacity to see the world, to
  love the world, increases exponentially with this gift of Love";
  2018-12-02-af-augustine asks that the new faculties be tended, "so tender,
  sensitive, and new. You must nurture these parts, protect them, love them,"
  and says what they give, authority to "your perceptions and what you speak
  of."
  Elaborations, which road opens them and how far. 2016-10-16-af-augustine
  removes the ceiling, the Celestial spirits themselves still growing, "we too
  are upon a journey of understanding just as you are but we are a little
  further ahead than you"; 2017-01-04-af-james gates the knowing of truth to
  the Love and describes it from within, the Spirit of Truth residing "within
  all who receive Divine Love" and giving "a signal, a sense, a knowing that
  you have discovered the truth," a rightness unlike the mind's agreement,
  with truth once perceived never dislodged, and the order fixed, "You cannot
  put the cart before the horse, my son. You must pray first, earnestly for
  the blessing of this Love"; 2019-01-21-af-augustine gates the deeper faculties
  to the Divine road, "a place of deep joy and recognition that can only come
  through the gifts and faculties of your soul," where the natural road lacks
  "the depth and acuity of the knowings of the soul."
  Chrysalis, anchor: 2018-10-16-af-seretta-kem, the gifts as seeds in dry
  chambers, "A soul that is unawakened by God's Divine Love has many chambers
  within it that are dry and inactive... Within each of these chambers are
  seeds," germinating as the Love reaches them, each soul unique as a
  snowflake, and the whole a kaleidoscope whose progression "is infinite."
  Witnesses. 2015-10-12-af-augustine, the expansion without end, "This
  awareness, this consciousness goes on for all eternity";
  2016-12-12-af-augustine, sensing God's Will, "the faculties that enable you
  to hear and see and understand in a clear way what that will is";
  2016-12-21-af-alec-gaunt, the perceptions flowering as the Love ignites the
  gifts, opening "doors of perception, gifts of healing and communication";
  2017-01-02-af-jesus, the soul's sight and knowledge coming "to your conscious
  self," "not of the mind but a knowing of the soul";
  2017-05-11-af-jesus, the awakened soul known by what it does, "a soul
  awakened knows far greater than the greatest minds on your planet";
  2017-07-16-af-seretta-kem, gifts of perception beginning as the light
  grows, "you feel waves of insight and emotion, of understanding and
  knowing";
  2017-08-21-af-matthew, feeling as a faculty of the soul, "a soul can feel
  its understanding, its comprehension of a Truth"; 2018-05-12-af-luke, the
  natural road's bounded development, souls who "refine their gifts in as far
  as their gifts are possibly recognized and expressed";
  2018-05-17-af-augustine, "You will be able to see others for who they are";
  2018-12-17-af-mary, "The soul has its own faculties and abilities to
  perceive and reason and know truth."
  Fences. Against `mind-vs-soul`: that thread argues that the two
  consciousnesses differ in kind and only one can know God; this one argues
  what the awakened soul can do. Against `knowing-your-own-soul`: that thread
  treats the soul as something to be known; this one treats it as something
  that knows. Against `divine-love-mediumship`: 2016-10-16-af-augustine has
  the spirits augmenting the seeker's perception, where the two threads touch.
  Against `two-paths`: 2019-01-21-af-augustine's road-gating passage also
  holds a role there.
  Structural note: Josephus has some faculties present in everyone and refined
  by the Love, "some of which do not require the awakening of the Divine Love
  but are naturally a part of the individual," while 2019-01-21-af-augustine
  gates the deeper faculties to the Divine road. The two fit if the natural
  faculties are refined and the deeper ones opened only by the Love; read them
  together at build.
