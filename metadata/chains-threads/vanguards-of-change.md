### vanguards-of-change

- `vanguards-of-change`: running from the commission itself to the freedom it
  requires.
  Foundation, presumptive anchor: 2016-01-08-af-jesus establishes the
  commission. "You are the vanguards," said twice; God "will open the doors"
  in the timing of His Will; their souls already sense what is coming; and the
  commission serves "that great plan of our Heavenly Father's for the
  salvation of mankind."
  Elaborations. 2016-03-06-af-jesus adds the requirement the commission
  imposes, that material burdens be released to have "the capacity and the
  flexibility to walk wherever God will guide you," compressed as "in this
  world but not of this world," with the change arriving "wave upon wave" and
  the vanguard title tied directly to the unburdening;
  2016-05-12-af-alec-gaunt turns the release into practice, mobility at a
  moment's notice and clears the fear objection, "No one will suffer from
  this. All those you love will be taken care of," with sacrifices that are
  "not truly sacrifices," closing on the exemplary logic of deployment, "How
  can the world change if you do not change?"; 2016-08-27-af-augustine extends
  the release to the seeker's own forward plan and makes the day the unit of
  readiness, "you must not project your intentions too far into the future in
  a specific way but to be open and flexible realizing that God has a plan for
  you that unfolds with each day," with the practice attached, "be open to
  whatever invitations come your way this day." It treats one's own intentions
  as an encumbrance of the same kind as possessions. 2016-12-05-af-augustine
  names the comfort of the circle itself as something to leave, "It is easy
  for you to envelop yourself in your own little worlds, safe, filled with
  love and comforts, joy and peace. Yes, my beloveds, this is the easy part,"
  since "if you are not out amongst mankind there is no way that God can use
  you as a channel to touch others," and gives the time together a purpose
  beyond itself, "to replenish and to heal, to strengthen and to teach."
  2017-10-25-af-yogananda meets the commission as one concrete invitation, a
  journey to India, and names the weariness that holds back the going, "Some
  of you are feeling taxed by the commitment you have made already," with the
  doors opening on the circle's readiness, "When you are ready to take up the
  mantle, to be brave and faithful, doors will open," and the cost of holding
  back falling on the service itself, "when you put limitations on God, you
  put great limitations on yourself."
  Objection-removed: 2016-12-10-af-james clears the belief that answering the
  call costs the people who depend on you, "This does not mean that your life
  will become chaos and those about you and who rely on you and who love you
  will be hurt and distraught and you will sacrifice your love for the love of
  God, no this is not the case," with the practice for those left uneasy,
  "for those you love who will be afraid of your choices, you must ask God to
  enfold them and care for them," and the promise that the change carries them
  along, "you will bring those who are with you along into that greater
  light." 2016-05-12-af-alec-gaunt gives the same assurance in one sentence
  inside its Elaboration; this message makes it the argument.
  Witnesses, the commission asserted without the release.
  2016-08-11-af-alec-gaunt has it materializing in the present tense, "You
  will travel the world to bring these truths to mankind and now you see this
  coming about"; 2016-08-29-af-faith-nyquist asserts commission, release and
  mobility together, "you step upon a great threshold of service to God... You
  have sat in prayer for many years. Some of you know it is time to put prayer
  into action," without a facet the 2016-08-11 witness does not already carry;
  2016-09-04-af-augustine states it as a travel programme rather than a
  readiness condition, "For we will be calling you to many places in the
  world," with "you come as missionaries" naming the role and no release asked
  for; 2016-10-15-af-confucius gives it in collective form, a place for each
  willing soul in an expanding work, "you will find your place within this
  work"; 2016-11-06-af-jesus restates it with the opened-door promise, "God
  will take you by the hand and show you the way, open the doors and connect
  you with all the loving souls," which is what Jesus said in
  2016-01-08-af-jesus; 2016-12-01-af-john-the-beloved, "the doors will open
  and you will find your way as disciples of the Master," for souls "destined
  to be lights in the world"; 2017-02-12-af-augustine, travel to knit the
  network, "God has entrusted you with this job, this role," and "to travel
  in order to forge those connections"; 2017-03-27-af-james, "you are at the
  front of this line, this wave of change, and this is not easy";
  2017-04-20-af-augustine, "surprising opportunities, unanticipated, that will
  allow you to take this leap forward in a very overt and recognized way";
  2017-07-09-af-matthew, the circle "guided to those places in this world
  where you are needed"; 2017-07-30-af-seretta-kem, the circle sent "into the
  parched lands of this world"; 2017-08-01-af-augustine, the calling put as a
  question, "Will you accept God's calling?";
  2018-06-18-af-matthew carries the vanguard title as its door, "You are the
  vanguards seeking to move forward... into a new dawn of Love and Truth that
  God intends for this world," with the circle cast as explorers rather than
  as recipients.
  Witnesses, the release half. 2016-08-15-af-alec-gaunt names the attachments,
  "You are still attached and longing to be loved by others, to be
  acknowledged, to have the material security that the world offers," set
  against the commissioning to publications, talks and Circles of Light;
  2016-09-16-af-john-the-beloved asks for the release of one's own needs, "to
  walk this world unencumbered" and "to set aside your own needs so that God
  may use you in purposeful and unencumbered ways in this world," a step
  further in than possessions or plans; 2016-11-27-af-augustine promises the
  travel in the spirit's own voice, "We will take you to many places and show
  you many things and connect with you many souls," with the release named as
  one of resistance rather than of material burdens, "as you lay down your
  resistance and completely embrace the Will of God";
  2017-01-16-af-keea-atta-kem, the safe circle left at times, "it would be
  easy to enwrap your group in a safe cocoon of love," with the wish granted,
  "there is nothing untoward in this desire," and the turn outward, "Then
  there are times when you must reach out to others."
  Witnesses, the reach of a small company. 2015-06-15-af-joseph-smith gives it
  a long range, "the reverberations of your efforts will spread through time
  throughout this world. Many generations to come will feel the effects of a
  handful of true souls who sought to be channels of Love," with the circle
  named as first-comers, "It is coming to mankind and you are the harbingers";
  2016-05-29-af-aman names the company itself, "You who are attempting to turn
  this tide are brave and blessed warriors who are moving in Light given by
  God to assist you in your efforts to bring change"; 2016-12-21-af-alec-gaunt,
  "It is dependent upon you and the few others that God will use to bring
  this light and truth and change to the world."
  Witness, the regathering. 2016-08-26-af-jesus has God, who "continues to use
  you and bring you together in groupings for specific purposes in the
  world... in ways and means that may seem impossible to you at this time."
  Held at witness because it rests on one forward-looking sentence in a
  message whose spine is the salvation plan; it could be promoted at build.
  Sightings, kept for the boundary they mark. 2016-09-03-af-james has the
  doors-opening claim in general form, "God will use you in ways yet
  unanticipated, opportunities will come your way so that you may be a change
  agent in the world," the commission and the opening without the release
  requirement or the mobility the thread turns on; 2016-09-08-af-jesus
  delivers the commission to one person in one named land, "I have sent you,
  my brother, to be my disciple of Truth in this land," where this thread
  concerns a group commissioned to mobility and this is a single resident
  commissioned in place.
  Fences. Against `preparation-to-be-a-rock`: rock is stability for what comes
  to you, vanguard is mobility for where you are sent. A message may serve
  both, as 2016-05-12-af-alec-gaunt does, but membership here requires the
  commission-and-mobility argument and not readiness alone.
  What is awaited. Whether the encumbrances form a series or one idea
  restated: material burdens at 2016-03-06-af-jesus, one's own intentions and
  plans at 2016-08-27-af-augustine, one's own needs at
  2016-09-16-af-john-the-beloved, the safety of the loving circle at
  2016-12-05-af-augustine. If they are three claims the last is promotable; if
  one, it stays a witness. 2017-01-16-af-keea-atta-kem restates the circle
  step and softens it rather than carrying it further: the cocoon is not
  wrong, and leaving it is a matter of times. 2017-03-27-af-james restates
  the plans step, "When those well laid plans that you make do not come to
  fruition," without carrying it further. 2017-04-20-af-augustine joins it to
  security, "ideas and dreams that I have concocted to give me a sense of
  security," which leans toward one idea restated. 2017-10-25-af-yogananda
  names the mind's weighing of feasibility, "burden yourselves with assessment
  of the possibilities of success," which sits with the plans step.
  Structural note: 2016-01-08-af-jesus also carries a `healing-channel`
  witness for its "great channel of service."
