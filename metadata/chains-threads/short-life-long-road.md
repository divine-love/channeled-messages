### short-life-long-road

- `short-life-long-road`: running from how short an Earth life is to how much
  it decides about the road the soul walks in spirit.
  Foundation, anchor: 2017-05-21-af-augustine. The soul takes with it only
  "your soul and your spirit," with "those acts of Love, a generous heart, a
  life well lived in the flow of Love." Nothing is left behind, "For you carry
  every thought and action within you," so "Your capacity to love directly
  informs where you will be in the world of spirit when you pass." The Love
  changes the road, letting the soul "sidestep many layers of spirit that are
  not of Light."
  Witnesses. 2015-08-20-af-james-padgett, "this short life on Earth is but a
  stepping stone to greater life"; 2016-05-02-af-andrew, "your journey of life
  upon this Earth-plane is short but has a great bearing upon where you will
  continue life in spirit," with the soul that prays for the Love "creating for
  yourself a wonderful heritage of Light"; 2019-02-10-af-augustine, the place
  the soul enters in spirit "determined by the condition of your soul," a place
  that "will reinforce your own condition." Read 2019-02-10-af-augustine with
  2019-01-31-af-yogananda, who says progression in spirit "can be swift indeed
  once there is a desire and the knowledge to do so": the two agree, since the
  road is swift for the soul that knows the way and long for the soul that
  arrives without it.
  Fences. Against `death-transition`, which is about what death is; against
  `two-paths`, which is about the two destinations; against
  `redemption-universal`, which is about every soul arriving in the end. This
  thread is about how the choices of an Earth life set the length of the road.
