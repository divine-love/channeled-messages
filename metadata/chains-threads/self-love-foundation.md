### self-love-foundation

- `self-love-foundation`: running from being loved by God, through loving
  oneself, to the love that then reaches others.
  Foundation, presumptive anchor: 2016-02-29-af-confucius carries the whole
  argument in one passage. The ground, "You are precious to God, loved by God
  and you must start to love yourselves in this way"; the turn outward, "For
  when you can truly love yourselves you can look upon yourself with
  compassion, you can allow yourself to feel compassion and love for others
  and to be a great a mighty channel of Love in this world"; and the failure
  state, "if you suppress those conditions within your soul, which restrict
  the flow of Love, and cover your own soulful longings for Love and to love,
  then you are stuck within this condition."
  Elaborations. 2016-10-17-af-keea-atta-kem routes the argument through
  perception rather than judgment, "As you begin to turn your gaze upon
  yourself and acknowledge how precious you are... in this way you then have
  eyes to see the beauty in others," so what is gained is the capacity to see
  another's beauty; 2017-07-06-af-seretta-kem makes self-love part of what
  makes the servant believable, "Part of the credibility that you must earn
  with those souls is the ability to love yourself," and names what blocks
  it, "your own thoughts that you are unworthy or a failure or that you do
  not love yourself"; 2019-04-05-af-augustine states it as logic from the other
  side, "Come to understand yourself above all, to accept yourself above
  all... In this way, how can you judge another when you are being of love and
  acceptance, joy and light?"
  Objection removed, that one is failing for not measuring up, anchor:
  2017-07-16-af-seretta-kem names the habit of self-criticism, "looking at
  yourself as if not measuring up to what is asked of you and what is truth,"
  and clears it, "Beloveds, you live in this world and are human. You cannot
  expect to be an angel overnight." Patience with oneself then grows into
  patience with others, "as this grows within you, you will feel greater
  tolerance, acceptance and love for those around you."
  Reframe. 2017-08-07-af-augustine turns looking after oneself from taking
  away from the work into honouring God. The generous servant who "thinks
  more of others than himself" and "does not leave much in reserve" is
  asked for "a balance of receiving and giving, and of loving oneself,"
  because "Love must be given to all aspects of your life, including
  yourself," and loving oneself is a way of saying to God, "I honour my
  existence, the creation that You have made, my dear Heavenly Father."
  Witnesses, the order of the loves. 2014-12-01-af-john-the-beloved, "it is
  your challenge to love yourselves and in so doing to truly love your fellow
  man"; 2015-03-28-af-augustine puts being loved by God first, "you are truly
  loved by God and in this you will truly love yourselves. And in this you
  will love your brothers and sisters"; 2015-05-18-af-augustine, "The next
  step is to truly love yourselves"; 2016-08-26-af-brother-mandus, "when you
  love yourself completely you cannot but love all in this way";
  2017-06-15-af-augustine, "For a soul that truly loves itself and is embedded
  within this pure Love of God there can be no judgment"; 2017-07-10-af-augustine,
  being precious to God as the ground for how the seekers see themselves, "See
  yourselves as children of God, truly know this, believe it, embrace it."
  Witnesses, compassion in place of condemnation. 2014-12-15-af-mary, "you are
  willing to condemn yourselves for those imperfections which are within and
  this my children is not required"; 2016-03-11-af-luke, "You
  must have compassion for yourselves and know that you are not to blame for
  your condition. You are in a way a victim of these conditions";
  2016-05-09-af-augustine, "Do not condemn yourselves... know with a deep
  compassion for yourselves"; 2016-09-01-af-jesus, "forgiveness of yourselves"
  placed before forgiveness of others; 2016-09-11-af-francis-of-assisi, a
  practice against negative thoughts "placed within you," saying to oneself
  "you are worthy, you are loved, and you are beautiful"; 2017-01-11-af-luke,
  "You must be patient, you must forgive yourselves"; 2017-01-23-af-alec-gaunt,
  "Have patience and compassion for yourselves, for each other";
  2018-06-24-af-luke,
  "you must have compassion for yourselves, beloveds"; 2018-06-26-af-matthew,
  "Stop judging yourselves. Stop condemning yourselves and condemning others."
  Witness, the subject on its own. 2016-03-16-af-seretta-kem, a short message
  wholly on it, "I hope that someday you can truly accept this, how beautiful
  you are, and to love yourselves fully with great joy and true knowledge that
  you are loved."
  Witness, from the receiving side. 2016-11-07-af-andrew, "All that inhibits
  this blessing from flowing into yourselves in great abundance is that doubt
  within you, that you are loved." Whether the thread's argument admits the
  receiving side is an open question; if it does, this message bears on the
  anchor.
  Fences. Against `judgment-to-love`: that thread is what to do with the
  impulse to judge; this one is how a person regards themselves. They meet
  where self-acceptance makes judging others impossible, at
  2017-06-15-af-augustine, 2017-07-16-af-seretta-kem and
  2019-04-05-af-augustine. Against the
  `worthiness-as-gate` candidate in the holding pen: that is what a person can
  receive from God; this is how they regard themselves. Against
  `sensitive-souls-redemptive`: that thread is what sensitivity is for.
  Structural note: the anchor. 2016-02-29-af-confucius states every step of
  the registry spine, but inside a message about cleansing;
  2016-03-16-af-seretta-kem makes self-love its whole subject but does not
  argue it. Weigh the two at build.
