### affect-as-soul-marker

- `affect-as-soul-marker`: running from the feeling God's touch brings to why
  the feeling can be trusted as a sign.
  Foundation, presumptive anchor: 2017-07-30-af-augustine names the feeling,
  says why it can be trusted, and asks the seekers to look for it. They are
  asked to check what they feel, "Can you feel the effects of this Love upon
  your souls and your being?", the feeling is named, "a response of joy,
  excitement, and peace all at the same time," and the reason is given, "For
  the soul recognizes its Creator," with no need of the mind's explanations.
  Elaboration. 2016-04-12-af-andrew, a talk on joy, says what the feeling
  tells the soul, in the thread's own word, "joy is a marker. It tells you
  that all is right within you, that you are in harmony," with the joy coming
  with God's Touch, "for with His Touch of Love comes that deep feeling within
  your soul of joy."
  Witnesses. 2016-09-02-af-andrew, peace as God's own mark, "when you feel
  this great and deep peace you know that God has touched you... for His mark
  is the peace that passes all understanding"; 2017-07-16-af-goldie, the
  feeling as a knowing that the seeker is on the right way, "a vibrancy, a
  joy, a knowing that you are indeed on the right path".
  Fences. The thread is defined by its argument, not by the image of joy (the
  argument-not-image ruling). Joy the Love gives or promises does not belong,
  only a feeling offered as something to read: 2016-11-27-af-confucius and
  2016-11-28-af-goldie carry the image without the argument and are set
  aside. Against `bell-toll-instrumentality`: that thread is the joy others
  see and are drawn to; this one is what the joy tells the person who feels
  it. 2016-04-12-af-andrew holds both, on separate passages.
  Structural note: the anchor is a choice for build. 2017-07-30-af-augustine
  gives the reason the feeling can be trusted, in a few sentences inside a
  message on the two paths; 2016-04-12-af-andrew makes the marker its whole
  subject but gives the reason only as the joy coming with the Touch.
