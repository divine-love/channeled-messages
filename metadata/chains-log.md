# Candidate Thought-Chains, Working Notes

## Purpose

People treat these messages as scripture. A chain layer that traces a single
idea across the archive, in the order the angels gave it, with each message
read in full, is both a study aid and a safeguard against the teachings being
quoted in fragments out of context.

This document is where we NOTICE candidate chains as we process each message.
We are not building chains yet. We are gathering evidence so that when the
archive is complete (messages uploaded through 2026), the eventual chains can
be built once, against the full set, with the entire arc visible.

## Design decisions already made (for future reference)

- **Unit of linking:** the whole message (message-level anchoring), never a
  sentence or fragment. This forces whole-context reading and discourages
  proof-texting.
- **Structure within a chain (revised 2026-07-06):** a chain is organized as
  role SECTIONS, in the order the argument requires (the registry's
  "argument it traces" column is the spine), with each section holding ALL
  its qualifying messages in chronological order by `date` received.
  Rationale: the angels rephrase important teachings because they are not
  always absorbed the first time, so restatements are part of the revelation
  pattern, not noise, strong witnesses are included, not discarded, and
  multiple messages may share a role. Within each section, one message is
  designated the ANCHOR (the developed statement); the others stand beneath
  it as witnesses, chronologically. Chronology thus still carries the
  angels' intended ordering, one level down: argument order lives in the
  sections, timing lives within each section.
- **A chain is a PATH, not a BAG.** A collection is unordered membership
  ("all the Two Paths messages"). A chain is an ordered argument that builds
  ("Hell is not eternal -> the dark spheres are a condition not a sentence ->
  any soul there can still turn to God -> therefore no soul is beyond
  redemption"). If a proposed chain is just "every message tagged X," it is a
  collection, not a chain. The anchor-per-section rule is the guard that
  inclusion never collapses a path back into a bag: a section may hold many
  witnesses, but the path is walked anchor to anchor.
- **One message belongs to many chains.** Chains will be defined in their own
  files (a future `chains/` directory, one file per chain, membership only).
  Chains are NEVER stored in a message's own front matter. In-message
  "next/previous in this chain" navigation will be GENERATED by a script that
  inverts the chain index, so bidirectional navigation costs nothing extra to
  maintain once the chain files exist.
- **Terminology:** prefer "paths" or "doors" over "chain" in any
  reader-facing language, to avoid collision with the existing "Chain of
  Light / Lattice of Light" terminology. ("Chain" is fine internally.)
- **Build timing:** construct the real chains AFTER the archive is caught up
  through 2026. Until then, only notice and log here.

## Link-role vocabulary (controlled, use these exact terms)

When logging a message against a candidate chain, tag what role it plays in
that chain's argument:

- **Foundation**, establishes the premise the rest of the thread depends on.
- **Elaboration**, deepens or extends a claim already made.
- **Objection-removed**, clears a contrary belief that blocks the next step.
- **Reframe**, recasts an idea in a new light or corrects a misunderstanding.
- **Testimony**, a spirit's first-person experience offered as evidence.
- **Chrysalis**, the transformational turn: once an objection is cleared or a
  premise laid, the chain opens a glimpse of what God's Divine Plan holds for
  the soul who walks this path. Forward-looking and revelatory. Distinct from
  Reframe (which changes how an idea is *seen*) and Testimony (which offers
  *evidence*); the Chrysalis shows the transformed vista the cleared ground
  now makes visible, the brick wall turned to glass.
- **Capstone**, the conclusion the thread builds toward. Distinct from
  Chrysalis: a Capstone *closes the argument*; a Chrysalis *opens a vista*. A
  chain may end on either, or carry both.

A message may carry different roles in different chains.

### 1963-12-26-ds-mary | Jesus' Birth and Youth | 1963-12-26
- `who-jesus-was` **[Foundation]** : The earliest message in the archive, and the earliest in any chain. Mary corrects the record of her son's birth and nature as the one person present for it: "Most of what the New Testament says about me is untrue." There was no virgin birth. Jesus was "born exactly like other babies." Joseph was a young husband, not the aged figure of tradition. There were eight children in the house. From this she gives the thread its central claim: Jesus became the Messiah rather than being born one, because God's Love "permeated his soul... made him, in time, His real, only begotten son." She also reports that his own family thought him insane, corrects the idea that his death was a blood sacrifice, and mentions that his natural love absorbed the ordinary turn toward marriage, which the complete-soul teaching later takes up (2019-01-27). -- Foundation-section member; the anchor choice is a three-way build decision between 1963 Mary, 2018-11-26 Jesus, and 2019-01-27 Jesus.

The thread's argument, lived rather than taught.

### 2015-03-24-af-augustine | There Is Fate and Yet There Is No Fate | 2015-03-24
- `free-will-and-gods-plan` **[Foundation]** : Given in New York the day after the March 23 gathering there, to a small circle beginning something new. Augustine sets out the premise the thread rests on: "there is fate and yet there is no fate." God holds an intended outcome and sees the whole of it, "God does see all and anticipates all." But free will pushes against that outcome constantly: "the power of free will buffets the enactment of God's will, turns and twists this flow." God does not answer this by overriding it, and Augustine is explicit that the constraint is legal rather than practical: "the laws of cause and effect, of free will, of creation, must be obeyed." What follows is a delay rather than a defeat. Outcomes are "not altogether predictable nor can they manifest in a predictable order," and "there is an end result which may take many, many years and generations and lifetimes to bring about." Instead of overriding, God works through willing agents, inspiring "His angels and the spirits and the many who wish to serve Him" to arrange events, which "may take circuitous routes and more time than anticipated and different players." What shortens the route is a willing soul that has been lit: the enactment "is not obscured by a lack of understanding of the truth, it is not obscured by a soul that is not ignited by God's Love," and "together the enlivened soul and the willing spirit may bring about miracles." Core Teaching.
- `free-will-and-gods-plan` **[Foundation]** : The delay is chosen rather than imposed. Augustine states the greater power without qualification, "the power of God's Love is greater than all the power of man's will," and in the next breath reaffirms that the laws must be obeyed anyway. God is not held back by free will; God declines to use a power He has. -- the difference between a limit and a restraint, and worth stating that way when the chain is built. Absent from the back-search entry.
- `free-will-and-gods-plan` **[Foundation]** : What the argument costs, and what it is for. Augustine names "the injustices and the extreme perversions of life, and the innocence of those who are the victims of the great power of mankind's free will, and the sin and error mounted one upon the other for generations," and then locates the remedy in souls who are "unencumbered by these conditions" and "strong enough because they possess the greatest gift possible." The delay is what allows the suffering to continue; the willing soul is what shortens it. -- the practical stake of the whole thread, and no other member states it this directly. Merge with the lines above at build, or keep separate if it carries better alone.

### 2016-11-28-af-luke | Luke Welcomes Those Who Are a Part of the Punalu'u Gathering | 2016-11-28 [not publicly posted]
- `spiritual-fellowship` **[Elaboration]** : Luke states the exchange as an obligation running both ways, at the moment a circle is being formed: "each of you has something to give to your brothers and sisters here and each of you has something to learn from your brothers and sisters in this circle." ### 2016-11-28-af-moses | Unite in the Name of Love | 2016-11-28 [not publicly posted]
- `spiritual-fellowship` **[Elaboration]** : Moses names what stops a fellowship forming at all, and it is the mirror of the failure this chain already records. "when you are bogged down in judgement, in fear, in assumptions, this cannot happen. You will have pockets, several here, several there but not unity, not true brotherhood and sisterhood." He states what is required, "you are required to give each a choice in a loving way, to open the door in a loving way and say 'you are welcome'," and he accepts that some will not come without forcing them, "Invariably there will be some who cannot and will not release those conditions... I am not suggesting that you accept those in a forcible way." He offers his own commission as evidence that it can be done: "I was charged by God to bring together the Jews, my people, and there were many such conditions and strife within that family; much larger than your own, and yet we did bond together, we did unite, we did put aside our differences," closing "no challenge is too great when God has his hand in your life." In 2015-06-15-af-joseph-smith the failure is belonging bought at the cost of truth. Here it is belonging that never forms, because judgment and old slights keep people apart, and the cost is named for the work rather than for the individual. This and 2016-11-28-af-luke were given the same day to the same circle and divide the subject cleanly, Luke on what fellowship gives and Moses on what prevents it; weigh them together. Testimony is a defensible alternative reading for this message, on the strength of the commission paragraph.
- NOTE (witness, `judgment-to-love`): the cost of judgment stated as a loss rather than a diagnosis, "When you push your brothers and sisters away in judgement or fear, you push God away, you push love away," with the positive form following, "When you envelop and accept, when you make an effort to go beyond your own barriers and reach out to others in sincerity, in humility, you bring the light to you." 2017-06-15-af-augustine holds that judgment is incompatible with Love; this says the brother and God go out the same door together.
- NOTE (sighting, `earth-changes`): "the changes that are coming in this world" in a subordinate clause, and "as time grows short, it is time for action."
- NOTE (considered and set aside, `comfort-dulls-longing`): "You are richly blessed. You are given a feast for the soul. God nurtures you at every turn." That candidate holds that met needs produce too little longing. Moses uses the same abundance as the ground of an obligation to give more, which is the opposite move.
