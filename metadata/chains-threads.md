# Candidate Thought-Chains, Working Notes

## Purpose

People treat these messages as scripture. A chain layer that traces a single
idea across the archive, in the order the angels gave it, with each message
read in full, is both a study aid and a safeguard against the teachings being
quoted in fragments out of context.

This document is where we NOTICE candidate chains as we process each message.
We are not building chains yet. We are gathering evidence so that when the
archive is complete (messages uploaded through 2026), the eventual chains can
be built once, against the full set, with the entire arc visible.

## Design decisions already made (for future reference)

- **Unit of linking:** the whole message (message-level anchoring), never a
  sentence or fragment. This forces whole-context reading and discourages
  proof-texting.
- **Structure within a chain (revised 2026-07-06):** a chain is organized as
  role SECTIONS, in the order the argument requires (the registry's
  "argument it traces" column is the spine), with each section holding ALL
  its qualifying messages in chronological order by `date` received.
  Rationale: the angels rephrase important teachings because they are not
  always absorbed the first time, so restatements are part of the revelation
  pattern, not noise, strong witnesses are included, not discarded, and
  multiple messages may share a role. Within each section, one message is
  designated the ANCHOR (the developed statement); the others stand beneath
  it as witnesses, chronologically. Chronology thus still carries the
  angels' intended ordering, one level down: argument order lives in the
  sections, timing lives within each section.
- **A chain is a PATH, not a BAG.** A collection is unordered membership
  ("all the Two Paths messages"). A chain is an ordered argument that builds
  ("Hell is not eternal → the dark spheres are a condition not a sentence →
  any soul there can still turn to God → therefore no soul is beyond
  redemption"). If a proposed chain is just "every message tagged X," it is a
  collection, not a chain. The anchor-per-section rule is the guard that
  inclusion never collapses a path back into a bag: a section may hold many
  witnesses, but the path is walked anchor to anchor.
- **One message belongs to many chains.** Chains will be defined in their own
  files (a future `chains/` directory, one file per chain, membership only).
  Chains are NEVER stored in a message's own front matter. In-message
  "next/previous in this chain" navigation will be GENERATED by a script that
  inverts the chain index, so bidirectional navigation costs nothing extra to
  maintain once the chain files exist.
- **Terminology:** prefer "paths" or "doors" over "chain" in any
  reader-facing language, to avoid collision with the existing "Chain of
  Light / Lattice of Light" terminology. ("Chain" is fine internally.)
- **Build timing:** construct the real chains once the messages through 2026
  are all in the archive. Until then, only notice and log here.

## Link-role vocabulary (controlled, use these exact terms)

When logging a message against a candidate chain, tag what role it plays in
that chain's argument:

- **Foundation**, establishes the premise the rest of the thread depends on.
- **Elaboration**, deepens or extends a claim already made.
- **Objection-removed**, clears a contrary belief that blocks the next step.
- **Reframe**, recasts an idea in a new light or corrects a misunderstanding.
- **Testimony**, a spirit's first-person experience offered as evidence.
- **Chrysalis**, the transformational turn: once an objection is cleared or a
  premise laid, the chain opens a glimpse of what God's Divine Plan holds for
  the soul who walks this path. Forward-looking and revelatory. Distinct from
  Reframe (which changes how an idea is *seen*) and Testimony (which offers
  *evidence*); the Chrysalis shows the transformed vista the cleared ground
  now makes visible, the brick wall turned to glass.
- **Capstone**, the conclusion the thread builds toward. Distinct from
  Chrysalis: a Capstone *closes the argument*; a Chrysalis *opens a vista*. A
  chain may end on either, or carry both.

A message may carry different roles in different chains.

## How to read this file

This file holds four things, and the folder beside it holds a fifth:

- **Theme Registry**, the scannable index of every minted thread, each with a
  stable slug, a one-line description, and the argument it traces. Check here
  before minting a new thread to avoid duplicates.
- **Thread rosters**, one per minted thread, holding its members in role
  order with the facet each contributes, its fences, and what it is waiting
  for. The rosters are not in this file. Each is its own file in the
  `chains-threads/` folder, named for its slug: the roster for `soul-expiation`
  is `chains-threads/soul-expiation.md`. Every minted thread has a roster
  file, and minting a thread means adding its registry row and its roster file
  together.
- **Holding pen**, candidate threads that have been noticed but not yet minted,
  each with its sightings and the development still needed before it earns a slug.
- **Patterns recorded and not penned**, observations that recur but are not
  candidate threads, kept so they are not noticed twice.
- **Open questions**, larger structural questions to revisit when building begins.

The per-message evidence (which message joined which thread, in chronological
order) lives in the `chains-log/` folder, one file per year of message. Its
rules are in the companion file **chains-log.md**.

The validator (`.github/scripts/validate_chains.py`) and the vault generator
read this file and the roster files together through
`.github/scripts/chains_io.py`, the rosters in alphabetical order by slug, the
same order as the registry. A roster file whose name is not its slug, a roster
left in this file, or a minted thread with no roster file fails validation.

Slugs are stable IDs. Once minted, a thread keeps its slug. When chains are
eventually built, slugs become chain filenames and the migration is mechanical.

A note on expected scale: chains are anticipated for essentially every subject
in the hierarchy, and likely for many collections and keyword topics as well.
Expect hundreds of threads. Mint slugs disciplined and check the registry
before adding new ones.

### Roster entry shape

Each roster is its own file, `chains-threads/SLUG.md`. The file opens with a
`###` heading carrying its slug, then a blank line, then the entry, and holds
nothing else. The entry itself is a single indented block, no blank lines and
no headings, with its internal structure carried by labelled sentence
openings.

The opening line names the thread and the arc it traces in one clause.

Role sections follow in argument order, each introduced by its role name as a
sentence opening: Foundation, Elaborations, Objections removed, Reframe,
Chrysalis, Testimony, Capstone. The anchor is named in the section it anchors.
Within a section, messages run chronologically, separated by semicolons, each
with the facet it contributes in a few words. Where a section grows too large
to read as one run, it is subdivided by facet group and the groups are named;
the groups subdivide one role section and do not add a structural level.

Then, as needed and in this order: Witnesses, for witness-level notes worth
recalling at build, grouped by the facet each supports. Fences, against the
threads this one is most likely to be conflated with. What is awaited, for
conditions the material itself has raised. Structural note, for anomalies a
builder would otherwise read as gaps.

Anchors are marked in the section they anchor. A message named in a roster
as a member or witness should have a log entry that agrees with it.

An entry explains the thread, never its own existence. Nothing is recorded
about why the entry was written, when it was filed, or when material was
added to it; later additions are folded into the role section they belong to.

An entry states what its thread argues, never how much of it has been found.
The archive is mutable: messages arrive out of order, at both ends and in the
middle, and role assignments stay provisional until a chain is built. Any
claim that depends on the archive's current composition, or on a role that has
not yet been fixed, will become false without anyone editing it.

So: no counts of members, roles, messages, witnesses or spirits; no spans of
years; no claim that something is the earliest or latest, whether in the
archive or in hand; no claim that nothing is logged between two points; and no
interval measured against a role, since "three weeks before the Foundation"
breaks the moment the Foundation moves. A number attached to messages named on
the spot is a reference rather than a size claim, and is fine.

An entry describes what each message says and what it contributes to the
argument. Where the relation between two messages matters, state the relation
itself, that one develops what another asserts, or answers it, or repeats it
without developing it, rather than the arithmetic between their dates. The
message_ids already carry the dates.

# Patterns recorded and not penned

Recurring observations recorded but not penned as candidate threads.

- **The lattice of Light, held as a layer rather than a thread.**
  chains-log.md records it as a structure across threads that `portals`
  connects to and feeds, so it is held as a layer and not penned.
  2016-09-10-af-andrew adds what joining the network returns to the person who
  joins: "Each time you pray you will benefit from this connection that you
  made this night. You will find that prayer will be easier, that there will
  be an opening to God in a deeper way." Elsewhere the network is the medium
  through which light travels outward to the world; here the benefit runs
  inward, the joining lasts, and it persists beyond the sitting. It fits
  neither `prayer-changes-world-conditions` (outward) nor
  `bell-toll-instrumentality` (radiance), which is also why it bears on the
  fence between those two recorded in Open questions: it is a third direction,
  belonging to neither. The same message draws the fence against `portals` in
  its own terms: portals attach to places and are conferred, while this
  network joins people and is joined by praying. 2016-12-21-af-jesus adds a
  direction between the members, the blessing of those who travelled reaching
  those who stayed, "for when you step forward, you all step forward, my
  beloveds. There is no distance between you." 2017-02-12-af-augustine gives
  the mechanism strand by strand: a strand formed by "this love, this
  connection, this soul recognition" between two people, reinforced by
  sitting together and by praying for the other "wherever they be in the
  world," energized by God, and joined into "a lattice that will encompass the
  world," which "will change the conditions of this world." That is the
  outward direction as well, which bears on the same fence. A decision is
  wanted at build time on whether the lattice becomes a thread or stays a
  layer.

- **Testing the truth against God rather than against other people.**
  2016-12-28-af-alec-gaunt, "Remember to test the truth. Do not be
  sidetracked with what another may wish to give to you in the way of
  guidance or explanation," with the pull toward borrowed authority named,
  "you are often vulnerable to strong personalities who have great
  convictions as to what is truth"; 2017-01-04-af-james, the soul's own test,
  "There is a sense of rightness when you come upon a truth," guarding against
  spirit explanations as well as human ones; 2018-08-06-af-solomon, "must test the
  truth in their own way"; 2019-01-30-af-jesus, "always test the truth that
  comes," there for what comes through mediums. It touches
  `divine-love-mediumship`, which locates verification in the soul, and the
  cost side of `spiritual-fellowship`, without belonging to either.

# Open questions

Larger structural questions to revisit when building begins.

- **The fence between `prayer-changes-world-conditions` and
  `bell-toll-instrumentality`.** Raised at 2016-09-04-af-augustine, which
  holds prayer drawing blessing inward and radiance reaching souls never met
  in neighbouring sentences, so both threads would claim the same passage. The
  curator's working direction, recorded as thinking in progress and not as a
  ruling: fence them by scale and by mechanism. `bell-toll-instrumentality`
  concerns what the Divine Love gathered in one person does to that person's
  encounters, so it works person to person. `prayer-changes-world-conditions`
  concerns the total effect of prayer on the whole. On that reading the
  Augustine passage splits cleanly: the inward-drawn blessing and its radiance
  belong to the bell-toll thread, and only prayer aimed at the world belongs
  to the other. To test the direction against before it is settled:
  2016-09-03-af-mary is a `prayer-changes-world-conditions` witness at a scale
  of one, intercession for a single named person, which is collective in
  neither scale nor mechanism; and 2018-11-29-af-abdullah-latif, an
  Elaboration on the same thread, makes the quality of one praying person the
  governing variable, which is the bell-toll thread's own move. Neither breaks
  the direction, but the fence will need a sentence about intercession aimed
  at one person, which belongs to the prayer thread by intent while looking
  person to person by scale. 2016-11-28-af-seretta-kem tests it in a
  different way: one sentence runs from what goes out from each person to the
  group multiplying it, "So much extends and expands out from each one of you
  and when you come together in groups, you increase the power of this
  exponentially." The direction splits the sentence at the "and", but what is
  multiplied is the same thing that went out, so the fence will also need to
  say how one flow is divided between the two threads. The lattice of Light
  (see Patterns) is a third direction that belongs to neither.

- **Consequences of two promotions.** 2016-09-09-af-andrew is an Elaboration
  in `simplicity-of-the-path`, and 2016-09-04-af-john-the-beloved is an
  Elaboration in `prayer-soul-longing`; see their roster entries. Andrew's
  mechanism widens what `simplicity-of-the-path` claims, from the path being
  simple to the seeker ceasing to complicate it. John the Beloved's floor
  comes with a rate, "the more effort you apply in this journey, the faster
  will be the awakening", which a reader can hear as undoing the floor it sits
  beside.

- **A ruling wanted: a minted thread's image returning with the opposite
  meaning**, from 2016-09-09-af-francis-of-assisi. The argument-not-image
  ruling settles argument against image for holding-pen candidates. This is
  the mirror case: a minted thread's own image returning from the same spirit
  with its meaning reversed. It bears on how the `fear-of-full-immersion`
  Reframe is worded. The role question is closed (the message is recorded in
  the `fear-of-full-immersion` roster as a Chrysalis-shaped sighting, with the
  reasoning there); the wording question is open.

- **A possible `free-will-and-gods-plan` connection.**
  2016-03-13-af-faith-nyquist ("Keep the Flame Burning") echoes the thread's
  circuitous-route image, "God's Will will manifest. It may take a circuitous
  route but it will be done." An encouraging echo rather than a development of
  the argument, so not logged as a member; confirm or set aside when a message
  develops the circuitous-route claim.

- **Messages that may need anchoring below the whole message.** Some long
  teaching messages move through several distinct ideas, which breaks the
  assumption that a whole message is the unit. Known case: 2015-03-24-af-augustine
  ("There Is Fate and Yet There Is No Fate") moves through fate and free will,
  the laws of cause and effect, angelic orchestration through circuitous
  routes, the willing soul as treasure, and the invitation to lost souls. It
  is anchored in `free-will-and-gods-plan` as Foundation, but its material
  close to the bell-toll thread and its "seek ye the Heavenly Father" material
  may belong to other threads below the message level. Its several
  `free-will-and-gods-plan` Foundation lines are covered by the log's rule for
  repeated lines (one role, passages read in order). Note other cases as they
  are found.

- **Children and the young: a collection, not a chain.** The theme reads as
  something to browse rather than a built argument. Seen: 2016-03-14-af-mary
  ("Talk on Children") and 2016-09-03-af-james ("Pray for the children"), one
  clause with no teaching attached, included for now with that reservation.
  The subject "Children & Future Generations" exists. A tenth collection (for
  example "Children & the Young") is a taxonomy decision for the curator, to
  raise as the material grows.

- **A possible "Life of Prayer" collection** (or "Foundations of Prayer"):
  messages that teach prayer itself as the foundation of the Divine Love path,
  which none of the current collections holds cleanly. Possible founding
  messages: 2015-11-28-af-faith-nyquist ("The Power of Prayer to Bring Change
  and Healing"), 2016-01-23-af-augustine ("The Power of Prayer"),
  2016-07-11-af-confucius ("Pray Daily", which argues the daily discipline
  specifically), and 2018-07-30-af-keea-atta-kem ("Prayer"). Like the children
  collection, a taxonomy decision for the curator.

- **Does `the-whole-requirement` belong inside `provision-for-service`?** The
  candidate is what is asked of the people; the thread is what God gives for
  a work that is His. 2017-06-06-af-seretta-kem puts the two together for one
  piece of work, the Direct Voice project, and makes the giving depend on the
  doing: the people are "to maintain a light about you and within you," and
  then "All the resources that are available to us are available to you".
  That is the thread's own argument, so the candidate may be one half of it
  rather than a thread of its own. Decide when a message shows whether what is
  asked can stand as enough without what is given.
