  resolving them. His heart understood the simple truths of Love and Grace,

  enthralled with his own image, worked in the ways of the mind, and did not

  frank about this. He says he is not entirely pleased with some of his own
  writings, that some of them missed the mark, and that the clarity he now has
  came only after death, as he traversed the spheres of spirit and entered the
  Celestial Kingdom.

  sanctuary at his former home, holding his own standard for the house: that
  it be a place of Light and healing where everyone who comes through the door

  that truth be spoken there plainly enough for anyone to understand. From

  concern for the ongoing work at the World Healing Centre and for the souls
