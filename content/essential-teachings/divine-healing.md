# Divine Healing

Messages that describe, demonstrate, or teach the mechanics of divine healing. Includes both accounts of healing events and instruction on how divine healing works — through prayer, soul purity, and the inflowing of God's Love.

---

| Message | Date | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [Healing Merkaba](../messages/2015/11/2015-11-28-af-seretta-kem.md) | 2015‑11‑28 | Seretta Kem congratulates the group for creating a powerful healing merkaba together and speaks of the great exponential flowering of interconnected Circles of Light in the world. He calls them to embrace all souls with tolerance and acceptance regardless of their beliefs or philosophies, assuring them that God will open door after door as they travel the world connecting with other circles of Light in God's great plan to bring healing and harmony to the Earth. |
| [Remember to Nourish Your Soul](../messages/2016/02/2016-02-10-af-confucius.md) | 2016‑02‑10 | Confucius counsels those doing healing work to pray for God to touch each soul through them, so that they act in resonance and harmony with the flow of Divine Love. He teaches that while many energies and emanations of body and spirit exist, the emanation of love from the soul is the most powerful, and that the healer must nourish their own soul foremost, keeping prayer and longing for God at the forefront so that all gifts, learnings, and healing fall into harmony. |
| [The Greatest Healing Energy of All Is God's Divine Love](../messages/2016/04/2016-04-13-af-seretta-kem.md) | 2016‑04‑13 | Seretta Kem acknowledges the many healing energies and practices present in the circle, but teaches that Divine Love, God's Essence poured into the soul through longing and prayer, is the one gift that cannot be passed from person to person, must come directly from God, and surpasses all other healing energies in power, permanence, and transformative effect. |
| [Expiation and Purification of the Soul](../messages/2016/07/2016-07-13-af-seretta-kem.md) | 2016‑07‑13 | Seretta Kem gives a lesson on expiation, explaining that as the Divine Love enters the soul it begins a cleansing in which the soul releases everything within it that is not of Love, and that the discomfort, heightened emotion, and bodily distress which follow are the effect of that release and not a sign that something has gone wrong. He adds that sensitive people absorb many energies from the world which must be released in turn, that purification reaches body and spirit as well as soul, and that the seeker's part is to welcome it and offer up something not in harmony with God's Love. |
| [Power of Prayers Multiplied in Healing Circles](../messages/2016/07/2016-07-25-af-seretta-kem.md) | 2016‑07‑25 | Seretta Kem teaches what happens when a circle prays for the healing of the world. Angelic healers and other circles of prayer rising through the spheres join the gathering, a portal of Light opens directly from God, and a vessel among them collects the healing energies and overflows into streams that angels divide and direct across the world, so that one beam of Light is multiplied exponentially and touches many thousands beyond those prayed for by name. He asks them to pray for the world's leaders and for all who pray, and that all who are touched be given the desire for God's Love. |
| [Questions Answered about Divine Love Healing](../messages/2019/01/2019-01-26-af-sri-yarisupta.md) | 2019‑01‑26 | Sri Yarisupta answers a question about healing energies, teaching that the universe contains a vast rainbow of healing gifts and that attempting to define, narrow, or ritualise them limits what God can bring through an open channel. He affirms that every soul has some degree of healing gift, and that receiving the Divine Love widens and deepens all healing capacities beyond what the mind can currently imagine. |
