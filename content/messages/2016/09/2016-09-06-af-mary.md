Beloved daughter, do not fear the hands of the physicians, they will heal you and we will be with them, guiding them. You will come to no harm, my daughter, and you will find complete healing.

My son, your heart grows restless. You seek change in your life and God will guide you through this forest to a place where you will truly know peace and happiness and in this way you will come to know your true gifts and express them for the good of all.

There is much work for all of you, beloved souls. There is much work, for mankind is on the verge of great change as this world is transformed, as God places His Hand upon this planet to bring change and healing and balance. And he desires that his emissaries of Love will help ease the fears and the confusion that will ensue from these changes.

You must all prepare yourselves. Listen to the callings of your heart. Know that God will use you to bring comfort to many and truth and love. There is so much work to do and you must, beloved souls, you must be close to God, for it is in this closeness, at-onement with God that you will be a mighty channel to bring healing and peace, truth and love. God needs you. God loves you. God will carry you. God will guide you and will love you, will always be by your side, always. Beloved souls, be with God. God bless you.
