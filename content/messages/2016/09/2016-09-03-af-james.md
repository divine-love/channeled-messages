There is my beloved souls, a great deprivation. Despite all the material that you possess, that is possessed within this world, almost all souls residing in this place have a great lacking within them and these conditions, this lack of love, this inability to reach beyond oneself in love, continue to create dark conditions, wars and strife, a deep hunger in humanity, beloved souls, a deep hunger for substance that is not of material but spiritual.

You have come together because you have recognized your own hunger and you have come together because you feel that in your association you may not only feed one another but feed your world in love. And this is the expression of your souls. This is the desire of your souls. And each soul that goes to God in innocence and of deep desire shall be filled up. Where there was lack, there will be joy. Where there is lack there will be wisdom. Where there is lack there will be love. And you must, beloved souls, venture forth into the world, seeking to love your fellow man, for there is deprivation all about you, beloved souls. Look into the eyes of those in the world and you will see it, you will see it – hunger, a desperation, a fear and a deep desire to be loved.

Pray for the children. Pray for those who are lost. Pray for your fellow man, for each soul in their own way, is lost and for this world to change each soul must find their way. For it is in that deep recognition of what it is that is needed that the world will change. And you all know and recognize the necessity for change in this world, for without a tidal change of attitudes, of priorities, of deep desire, the world will continue to darken to the point that all will unravel. You cannot, humanity cannot control the world. You add to the conditions, you add to the energies of this world in ways that are disruptive, that will bring chaos in time, and now is the time for each of you to look within and look about you and realize that without the Hand of God upon each one in this world there can be no solutions only further contributions to the difficulties and the darkness.

Mankind is lost, my beloveds. It is mankind you must pray for and it is your own souls that you must pray that you will find your way and for all those that you love, and for all those who are lost, you must pray that you shall all find your way in this darkened world to bring Light, to bring peace, to bring Truth and most and crucially, to bring Love.

This is what you must do. This is so very important and come together, beloved souls, in prayer as often as you may, to bring further Light to this place and to yourselves. Come together in Circles of Light and create further Circles of Light. This tiny droplet of Light will expand and expand in accordance with your efforts and prayers for this world and for yourselves and receiving the Divine Love will further enhance and bring a great blessing to all. You will know what to do as your souls grow in this Love. You will know what to do because what is necessary and required will be obvious as you look at the world through the eyes of your souls.

Beloveds, be strong. Focus on Love and Light. Focus on being a channel of Love and Light and God will use you in ways yet unanticipated, opportunities will come your way so that you may be a change agent in the world and bring further Light, some peace, some change and some Love. The opportunities abound. It is for you to see and to act, to put love into action, to put wisdom into application, to put Truth into every breath you take.

Thank you, beloved souls. I am James and I love you.
