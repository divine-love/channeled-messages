[Standing], when you feel this great and deep peace you know that God has touched you, you know that your soul has been awakened to some degree and that you are touched by God, for His mark is the peace that passes all understanding. Seek out this beautiful peace; seek God in this way and know that His Love comes with this great and gentle touch within your being. God bless you. I am Andrew.
