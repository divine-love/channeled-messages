God calls you all, my beloveds, calls you all from your slumbers to awaken, awaken your souls. In this way you may know the glory and the beauty, the grace and the power that lies within you awaiting the inflowing of the Father's Essence, that glorious Light to infill your souls and bring Light, an awakening Light, a Light that shall carry you ever forward upon your journey of knowing who you truly are, of knowing God in all His glory and beauty.

Open yourself, to this inflowing of God's Love. Walk in this Light, be in His Grace and all that troubles you, all that burdens you, all that is not of Love will fall away and it will be an awakening, a sense, a feeling, a joy, and a freedom. I beckon you all to these greener pastures of life, greater life, greater love, greater joy. Be with me, my beloveds, upon this journey and I shall lead you to those places of Light, those places of awakening, those places of joyful acknowledgement of your own beauty, your own wonderment, your own possibilities unique to each beautiful soul.

A soul that thirsts and hungers for the bread of life, beloved, [longs for] God. And I shall lead you, beyond the shadows into Light and love everlasting. Love everlasting, joy unceasing, a depth of knowing and understanding the truth that cannot be had by any other means but this glorious benediction within your souls, the Love of the Heavenly Father that will touch each and every one of you, my beloveds, and will awaken you and bring you deep and abiding peace. Drink deep, for this gift awaits your invitation, your longing and prayers to receive the holy communion, the loving Touch from God. You step into the Light, now open your eyes to the Light, open your souls to the blessing that awaits.

God bless you. I am Jesus and I love you. God bless you.
