I see my little birds have migrated back. Now, my beloved souls, you can not stay away from this place of Light, though you look around and wonder, but I tell you this is a place of Light and you are the custodians of the Light, beloved souls.

Continue to do this work, to listen to God's promptings and to go where God wishes for you to go, for how many carry this truth beloved souls? So very few. And how precious you are in this world to have the knowledge that you have, to have the experience that you have, and the Light within your souls is burning a true testimony of God's Love within you.

Beloved souls, for each who is willing and strong enough there is much work, there is much work. And the means will be provided, the opportunities will be laid before you to continue upon this journey, this caravan of hope taking you from one place to another, sowing the seeds of truth, bringing Love, and comfort, and healing to many.

Yes, you begin a great journey that will take you to many places. Beloved souls, allow God to carry you forth in this great work and as you step forward you will know what you are meant to do. So, my little birds, you perch upon this perch for awhile then fly to another and some will fly to others singing your songs of love. Continue, beloved souls, take your journeys, your flights, your love around the world. God bless you. May God always, always bless you. And yes the healing of Divine Love is a special blessing for all. God bless you.
