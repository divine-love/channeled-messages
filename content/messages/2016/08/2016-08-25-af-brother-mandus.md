God bless you, beautiful souls. I am Brother Mandus and I see that you carry your banner of truth. Each one of you comes here to bring the truth, the truth of Love and how Love redeems the soul, and how Love is the greatest power of all. And this truth you bring with your presence and the grace that flows all about you and through you. And this is what you carry, into my house and you are welcomed, heartily welcomed, into my home for I wish that my home be a place of Light, of Love, a place of healing, a place where all who enter the door are embraced with love, are cared for and are respected. That was always my philosophy and I wish for this attitude and this demeanor and this flow of Love to continue within this place.

And yes, we are pleased, all of us in the Celestial Kingdom who are a part of this venture are very pleased that you have made your efforts to come these distances, to be a part of this, to stand for this truth of God's Love. And you must stand firm, gently but firm. And allow God to work through each one of you, for each one of you here has a special gift and some ability to contribute to what is meant to be accomplished at this time together. I do not say that you will be aware of what it is that is accomplished at this time or how you play your part, but it shall flow through you. You will be inspired and I promise you that an angel will be with you at all times and will bring their comfort and their protection for you.

And yes, there's been talk of the great portal of Light within the sanctuary and this is your task, beloved souls, to help bring forth in more powerful ways this portal. The intensity of this Light will bring many manifestations of God's Will, and healing, and Love into this part of the world. And it is a great responsibility that we place upon you, beloved souls, to be focused, and prayerful, and faithful as you pray in this place.

Now you are to meet other souls there who are primarily within their mind, they have very set beliefs and are rigid and I would request that you do not confront these individuals in a way that would continue to harden their perspectives. No, you must embrace them in love. You see, what you are doing here will be repeated over and over again in the world. This is but one opportunity, and in many ways a test, for you to deal with these earthly conditions, the resistances, the attitudes, the politics of this world, and to be able to manifest God's will through you despite these attempts to block and divert your intentions and God's intentions. It requires that you listen carefully to your guidance, to be spiritually attuned, to tread softly, but to walk with your head high, to be clear in your own understanding of what it is you are doing here. For you've made great efforts, each one of you, to come to this place. God has entrusted you with a task. The doors are opened, the angels have worked diligently to bring about the conditions so that this Light may manifest in this place in a powerful way and this great blessing will benefit many, many souls; you will benefit, those present will benefit but there are many connected to each one of you that will benefit and many that you know not who they are will benefit from your prayers and this effort together.

You have sought to serve God; each one of you dedicated souls have chosen to serve God and now you are given an opportunity to do so. Do not let any negative obstructions within your minds inhibit what is meant to be. You must take the higher road, you are seeking the highest, beloved souls, and you must manifest the highest in everything you do as God's channels of Love.

And I wish to personally thank you, each one of you, for your decision to come here in love, to answer the call, to be a channel of Love and to allow love above all else to manifest in this work that you do. For what can be accomplished without love? What can be accomplished without prayer and faith and your intention for the highest?

Embrace one another, embrace all you meet, be in God's flow of Love and nothing will stand in the way of what is meant to be. God bless you, beloved souls, I am your brother Mandus and I am a resident of the Celestial Kingdom, I know the Truth, God bless you and keep you in His grace. I love you.
