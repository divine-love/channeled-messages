Brother Mandus here. How humbling it is for me to follow the presence of the master in your midst. And it is my wish that the Master Jesus may walk within this place freely and his truths may be taught freely within this building for they are the truths of love and liberation, of exploring and expanding of the soul, of expressing gratitude by bringing healing and peace and comfort to the lost souls in this world. And this is my wish, for this house was dedicated to God and it is to be used in God's service, to whomever may come through this door that a blessing will be given, a greeting, an embrace, that there be respect, that there be humility for the servants of God who come to minister upon the people.

This is my wish, that love be the foundation of this organization, that Light be infused in every nook and cranny of this building, that truth be spoken plainly so that all can understand, and that those who come are not intimidated, or turned away but are welcomed and given some nurturance, an embrace, an acknowledgement. For when you are truly a servant of God this is a part of this service, this is a part of love, and to espouse upon the values and understandings of esoteric religions falls at the feet of those who come for nurturance and comfort and healing. It has no effect; it is the power of love, the simplicity of a simple hello, I love you, that reaches the soul, that nurtures the heart, that brings what is needed truly for the lost and lonely souls in this world, and there are many, so very many.

How blessed you all are to know the Touch of God, to know this fellowship, to be strong in this Light, this Love. How blessed you are. But for every soul that is blessed in this way they must give of themselves in what way is a part of God's Will in the world. Unique to each individual. As to their gifts so shall they walk in the world. Do not hold back, do not hold back, my friends, but give of your heart. Be a channel of love in the world. Do not judge, but embrace and allow that love to flow freely to all you meet no matter that they express themselves in ways that you find difficult to understand or accept. No, my beloved, beautiful friends, you must accept all. For when this Love resides within you, the message, the transformation, that it brings is to love all and to love yourself completely and when you love yourself completely you cannot but love all in this way.

A goal is set when you receive God's Love, and that is, that your life and your beings may be filled with Love and nothing else but Love.

I found in my life that when I loved and when I gave and when I dedicated myself to service that these were the greatest times in my life, the happiest times, the most productive times, and the most blessed times. It was in service, it was in giving of myself wholly and freely to others that I found my great joy and fulfillment of my soul. And every person has a purpose, everyone has gifts, everyone can be used by God to bless others, to serve others. And this is my encouragement to you, my brethren, to walk that path of service, to be a channel of Love for others, to explore within yourselves those great potentials, the potentials of your soul that are awakened by love and as you journey in this way through your life, oh what can come, what treasures and riches you will accumulate within you, and all about you from these acts of love in the world, by embracing all.

I encourage you all, I encourage you all to express love in the world. Be courageous, be strong, be humble, be generous, and do not fear the consequences but embrace the opportunities that service brings in this world, and the blessings that being a channel of Love will bring within your souls. For as you give love so you shall receive love. As you allow that love to flow, so a part of that love is retained within you, accumulating within your soul, bringing enlightenment, true knowledge and understanding.

Beloved souls, I congratulate you on your efforts as you come together in love and continue to be together in love, to embrace all those who come through this door to come to pray with you, embrace them and pray with them and see through the eyes of your soul how precious they are, how loved they are by God.

Thank you for listening to me this day. I am so happy to be a part of your efforts and I continue to be with you as you gather and to pray with you as do many angels surround you, a great network supporting you in these efforts and this is beautiful to behold. As you continue to revitalize this great beam of Light that comes into this place and will affect many in ways of healing and bring change in miraculous ways, surprising ways. Continue to pray for the Light to build within this place. Continue to pray for the Light to build within yourselves and continue to pray that many will come through these doors and receive this great blessing which resides in this home. A blessing from God, a Gift to many, many souls. God bless you, beloveds. I am your Brother Mandus and my heart swells with love and appreciation for all of you who come in humility, who come and answer the call which God has sent for each one. God bless you.
