God bless you, I am Faith. Yes, you have come to a critical juncture in your efforts to bring God’s Will into my home and you are somewhat anxious because you, like us, see the window of opportunity that is laid before you and you wonder when to act and how to act. What are the barriers in your way?

And those that are a part of this, who reside within the building and have authority to make decisions are in various states of anxiety, concern and some are hopeful, even naive. And yes, part, a component of your efforts, requires a consideration of the material and I say that when you approach some of the trustees there will be a clearer understanding of how to proceed. Some advice will be given by the one who professes not to have any faith and there will be a solution, a solution where the resistance and the error will fall away and the true souls committed to God’s Will, will champion your cause and we would indeed advise that you act soon, for things are coming to a head and that very symbol of the breakthrough will come very quickly.

For you all see, all the parties see, that the writing is on the wall. There is a great crisis within this place and God is moving his hand upon each soul involved in this interplay, helping to bring a resolution, influencing and showing through inspiration and insight what is necessary. What will unfold will not be as difficult as you can imagine. You must not approach this with anxiety or fear but with faith and a clarity that says I am doing God’s Will and that as well you must use your gifts and abilities to play your part in order that change must come smoothly and in harmony, the rhythm of which is respectful to all parties, not a pressuring of those who are resistant but a presentation that makes good sense, is loving and respectful and will acknowledge the past and honor the present and accelerate the work and Light that will come in the future.

Names are not important for this organization. If this is an issue that is difficult to maneuver about, be gentle and kind; do not press your points; allow dignity and be joyful, beloved souls, that God is working so beautifully and powerfully in this arena and that each one of you feels the import of what is to take place that you are willing and flexible and are able to follow your guidance.

I say do not hesitate but walk with respect and love. Allow the other party to air their grievances, to put forth their ideas so that you may negotiate and come to an equitable solution, for when God moves in this way all needs are met, harmony ensues, love underbinds all actions.

And so you must approach in love and those who are dedicating themselves to the success of this will be mightily blessed, they will not sacrifice but gain much, for God blesses those who act on his behalf. And this instrument and some of you, as well, have seen visions of what is possible there, have seen in a prophetic way what will come of this beautiful place and the Light within it. And we have encouraged you mightily over the years to place your focus upon this place, to be a part of this grand scheme, this wondrous plan that God has for the salvation of mankind and this place has a role to play. It is a piece of the puzzle and as you incorporate this other pieces will fall into your awareness, actions, aspects of God’s plan.

An invitation will be given to enact, to accept each one has a role to play, each one will be given a great gift and all is dependent upon your efforts, your personal efforts to be close to God, to yearn and long for his Love, to continue to transform and heal you, for greater harmony, and deeper insights strengthen you and wherever you go and whatever others may say of you, beloved souls, you will be in the Light. You will not be hurt by those who would assail you. You will know great joy and many, many souls will be blessed by your efforts, for you will be a channel of God’s Love and Will in this world that is now and will continue to undergo great changes. And you will bring the Truth, you will demonstrate the Truth, you will teach the Truth, and you will minister to many, many souls who require comfort and healing, love and acknowledgement. You will turn their eyes heavenwards and they will see that they are loved.

Beloved souls, you step upon a great threshold of service to God. Do not hesitate, but walk through joyfully and sing your praises to God and acknowledge the true beauty and Light that lay within each one of you, beloved souls. You have sat in prayer for many years. Some of you know it is time to put prayer into action, love into action and to listen and acknowledge God’s Will within your life, where you may go, what you may do will be influenced by God’s Will. Precious souls you are ready. The time has come. The time has come. Walk upon this path, casting aside any doubt, acknowledging the deep faith that you have and letting go of all the barriers that have been clung to so resolutely. The time is now to release yourself to God, to walk naked as a child in trust to your Heavenly Father.

God bless you, beloved souls. We are with you. We will protect you. We will guide you. You will see how it will unfold in surprising ways. I am Faith and I love you and I am joyed by your efforts and willingness to walk bravely within the doors and declare yourselves as children of the Truth. God bless you. God bless you.
