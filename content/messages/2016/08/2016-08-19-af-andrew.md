As you venture forth in your desire to be a channel of Love for God, your travels will be blessed, and the angels will accompany you. They will clear the way for you and will make a path of Light for you to walk upon as you seek to serve, to love, to support your brothers and sisters and to bring the truth of God's Love wherever you go. The angels will be with you; God's hand is upon you as you well know.

Put aside your fears. Walk in faith. Do not allow any negative obstruction to hinder your progress in this regard. Be joyful. Accept the blessings that God has to give you. Do not judge or hinder yourselves with doubts, but walk with the firm knowledge that you are blessed and carried, that you are loved and guided. All will be well.

Yes, there are things you must do to prepare yourselves. There are material matters to be taken care of and this is your contribution to this effort, to prepare, to deal with those things in the world that must be dealt with in order to free yourselves to do this work. But God's Touch even is within this. God's blessing even is within this. As long as you do not allow yourselves to be wound up into concern and panic and fear but to walk through and do each chore that is necessary with grace and love, with joy. In this way you do not attract negativity. In this way you allow God's full Touch, within each thing that you do, to be present and to bring harmony.

It is difficult often to choose this way, to trust in this way, to allow God to perform His will through you and about you, and within you, rather than rely upon those old habits, the willful and the controlling. No, my beloveds, as you continue to journey upon this path, as you continue to place your faith in the guidance of the Heavenly Father and to know His Will you must put aside these old ways of worrying and stress. They are not of love; they do not contribute in a positive way to the outcomes that are meant to be. And this kind of strength, this trust and faith will bring what is required to you, in harmony, in fullness, in beauty. God will provide for your needs, my beloveds, as long as you enter into the flow of His Love and continue to wade ever deeper into this mighty river that will carry you, that will provide for you, that will protect you, and will guide you. God's hand is firmly upon you. Surely, you must feel this and know this. Is this not enough? To trust in this, to be at peace, to be in this flow of love?

Be strong, my beloveds. Do not allow the negativity, those shards and shreds of negativity that are within you to trigger the influence of negative in your life but to give this to God, to be at peace, to trust in this wondrous flow and to know with wisdom, to perceive in wisdom what is required and to act upon this with faith and trust.

I know it is difficult in your world. You are so used to having control, to being on top of things, as you call it, and I tell you God is in control and all you need do is listen and to comply with His Will. This new way of being is but another way you may enact the truth, is another way that you are shining examples of the truth, and many will look upon you in wonderment, for they will see in their lives from this perspective of control and logic, the mind dominating all and you, my beloveds, must allow your souls to be the proactive force within your lives and in this way God's Will will manifest the wondrous, beautiful ways.

Be at peace, beloved souls. Be at peace and know that each step you take carries you closer to God, unfolds your true purpose and brings you to greater harmony and Love. Each step you take in faith is a tribute to your Heavenly Father, is a gift of love to God. And this, my beloveds, will bring many rewards as you walk upon your path, as you continue to walk through many doors which God opens for you, as you release your fears, as you let go of your anxiousness and be at peace, beloved souls.

I am Andrew and I love you. God bless you. God bless you.
