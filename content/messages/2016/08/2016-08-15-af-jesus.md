What greater journey can there be in life than this sweet embrace, this unfolding of your souls in this special Light and Love? No, my beloveds, nothing can compare to this journey, this walk in God's beloved soul to be at one, to know, to truly know you are embraced and you will continue upon this journey embraced, carried, nurtured, blessed in so many ways. You carry the treasures of heaven within you. You are rich beyond your wildest dreams. Beloved souls, continue to allow the unfolding of your souls in this way and your eyes will open, your perceptions will show you the truths of God's universe, the power of Love, the great awakening within you will bring Light to many others.

Continue, continue and you shall be embraced like never before and you shall know Truth in such depth and power and clarity that you will never doubt again but walk with a surety and a grace and a joy that will be a beacon of Light for many others, who will be drawn and seek what you have and you shall share in your loving embrace the truth of God's Love and this flame will ignite the lost souls and bring a sweeping, cleansing flame throughout the world, an awakening.

So as you embrace God and embrace the truth so God will use you to embrace many so they too may know this and express this joy, this great Love. And so, the world will be healed one prayer at a time, one embrace at a time until there is a mighty transformation, an acknowledgement from the souls of mankind. For the souls of mankind long for this, yet so many, so very many do not recognize this longing, do not allow themselves to feel the deep aching within their souls for God. You must take their hand and say "Do not fear, do not fear. For when you acknowledge those deep, aching longings within you, you are indeed strong and will find what it is that you look for." This is your mission, my beloved souls, to bring the lost souls of mankind to this simple truth that will heal them and bring them comfort and joy.

Continue to walk this path and I shall be with you. I am Jesus and I lead you and I love you. God bless you, beloved souls, who walk with me in the Light of God's Love and Truth. God bless you.
