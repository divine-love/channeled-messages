I come, I am Jesus. This ground has been consecrated. It is hallowed ground. Continue to say your prayers within this place, this sanctuary of Love and Light. Continue to join with God bringing these blessings forth in this beautiful, hallowed place. It is the Will of God, my beloved souls, that you worship and commune and heal and pray in this place, set about by God as a special gift. Open your eyes, beloved souls, to the power of this Divine Blessing brought about by many prayers, said by many souls, brought about by the deep longings that have been expressed here, and the power of prayer is manifest in this place.

The Light of God’s Touch is in a great beam filled with healing and grace filled with many of God’s blessings cascades down upon you, filling this room, expanding beyond this room and this building and this place you reside within, reaching many. It is consecrated ground, beloveds. Honor this with your prayers, behold this with the eyes of your soul, feel the joy of God’s blessings upon you, beloved souls, and what can come of God’s Will and God’s Touch upon this Earth. And healings are possible within this place, revelations of the soul will open for those who are willing to see and to know. Great comfort will be derived by those who pray within this place, and the Christ spirit, the Divine Love will fill this place in great abundance. Do you recognize the gift that God has given to you?

And those of you who are custodians of this blessed, beautiful place, I urge you to acknowledge God’s Will and his gift to you and to the many who will come through these doors and pray together to receive, to receive in powerful ways opening to them greater faith, an awakening of their souls, a soothing and healing of their bodies, their spirits, their minds. This is a gift from God, beloved souls, that you can not turn away from but must acknowledge for it is a rare gift, a powerful gift, and an important gift for mankind.

I shall continue to make my presence known within this place, for with this gift of Love and Light, healing and peace, I may come in my presence of Love to assist you all as you heal in prayer. Open yourselves to what God desires to give to you and to all who come. Blessed souls, seek God. Know what true love is. Be humble. Listen. Be with God, and such great bounteous blessings will be yours in this beautiful place touched by God, filled with love. It is yours, a gift of love.

Thank you for your prayers, your efforts, and to all those who’ve gone before you, who have through their efforts and their faith and their love have come to draw God’s special blessing and dispensation upon this ground. God bless you. I am Jesus and I love you. God bless you.
