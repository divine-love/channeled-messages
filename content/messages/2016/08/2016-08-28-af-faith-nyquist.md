I am Faith and I greet you all in this beautiful room, a sanctuary of healing and peace where God’s presence touches the hearts of all who enter its threshold. And I greet my dear friend Anne, whom I love, and am with often, and Timothy and all my old friends who are still in this world.

The Will of God sweeps through this building and it carries many souls with it, souls who are destined to be here in this place, in prayer, in service, teaching, healing, and many are meant to come, of different faiths, some with varying ideas and concepts. Know these doors are meant to bring in all peoples, all races, religions, creeds, and sects. These doors are meant to allow the seeking souls, the curious souls, the loving souls, the souls in pain and confusion, all of humanity must be represented in these walls so that God may minister to them, that His Will be done in this place. And each of you who commit to bring forth your desire and intention to serve in a loving way, are welcome into these doors and this place. For what is a centre for healing for the world if you do not bring the world within and heal? What is God’s Will, if you do not embrace all in love?

And I know that those who have been here have found this a struggle, to draw in the souls who are meant to be within this place and I say, continue to pray for this miracle of Love to manifest. And I know that there are beautiful souls who come through these doors, who desire to pray, to give and to receive and this must build. For this place to continue to stand it must build and expand. As this Light that has come into this building has expanded and pulsated out, it will draw many, it will draw many. And I know that each one of you here can give of yourself in beautiful ways as God’s channels of Love. I know you are capable of embracing many and I encourage you for it is indeed God’s Will that healing and love, comfort and truth, be given in this place, God’s house, a house so beautiful.

Utilize the Light that has been given and all of you please pray for this place that it shall be a true center of Light, a shelter from the storm of life, a place of refuge, of healing, of nurturance. And each one of you have affirmed this purpose for this place through giving your love and your prayers, by embracing one another, by being that Light.

Please continue to walk in that Light of God’s presence, to carry that Love wherever you go, to be those bright and shining examples of Truth, to hold your head high, to walk knowing how beautiful you are, and how beautiful is each soul in this world, all children of God, and as such, your brothers and your sisters in the world.

There is much work to do, my brothers and sisters, there is so much work to do. And all work starts with your intention and I say the power of your prayers to bring about God’s Will manifest here, bringing love into action, and with each action love. Open your hearts. Open your minds to the possibilities. Open your souls to God’s Love and you cannot fail in your efforts to bring healing to the world, healing to the world. This is so important. And healing cannot come to the world without your intention and commitment and faith in this process, for each channel of Light and Love in the world must walk in faith and be clear as to what it is they are engaged within. As God guides you along your path, be clear, my friends, be focused, even resolute in your efforts to be that channel of Love in the world, to be used by God in whatever capacity is available to you.

Yes, there is much work to do and the time grows short and the workers few but with the power of God’s Love and God’s Will those who engage within this work will be empowered to change the world. Yes, the world needs to be set upon a different course, one in which love and respect, truth and honor, be the cornerstones of everyone’s lives. Be that, my beloved, beautiful friends. Express this in everything you do and be humble, be generous, and kind, love your brothers and sisters, seek God in all that you do, and listen to your guidance, for God has much to say to you. God will guide you. God will heal you. God will envelop you in his Love, and bring you to a place of deep and abiding joy and peace.

Beloved friends, it really is so very simple and as you live this simple life all the complexities will fall away or fall into place and you will not be bothered by those conditions of the world that are so difficult. You will know love, clarity, you’ll be strong, you will know what to do and you will hear God’s song within your heart and dance to this beautiful rhythm and it will be a joyful dance, a creative dance, a dance that only you, as a unique soul, will follow. God blessed you with life and I urge you to live it fully and the best way to express yourself is in giving to others in loving ways, to walk through life in this glorious expression of who you truly are. Be true to yourselves, be true to God, and love one another.

Thank you for listening to what I have to say. I want you to know how much I love you and I make effort with each one of you here to help you upon your life’s journey. My prayers, my love are with you. God bless you, beloved souls. I am Faith and I love you.
