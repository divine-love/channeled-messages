It is your friend Alec, and I wish to congratulate my two charges for their long and successful marriage and their continuation of this beautiful partnership, and I wish to say that I played my part in bringing these two souls together, and I want them to know that this union was planned even as they were children and how the angels worked to bring them together in this beautiful story where such beautiful souls were brought together by those loving servants of God who mentored them and prepared them, those in spirit and those in the flesh who worked and taught and encouraged their soul growth so that when that time came that they were together, eye-to-eye, they recognized within themselves this was a union blessed by God. And as you see this union has borne fruit. As you can see there is such a great complement of gifts and abilities amongst these two beloved souls that shall carry them ever further in service and is unfolding according to God’s Will and plan and this is merely the beginning of a long journey of service, a great effort on their parts and on the parts of we in spirit and empowered by God’s blessings to bring this Truth and Love to the world.

Yes, there will be many publications, many talks, many Circles of Light and much more. This will require an ever deeper commitment and effort on their part to walk this path in accordance to God’s Will, to be always attuned, to be always willing and ready to enact the guidance given to travel, to embrace, to be generous and loving at all times.

And in this service, which does involve some sacrifice, there will be great rewards, beloved souls. And I say this not to these two beloved souls but to all of you; there will be great rewards. The key is in listening and enacting God’s Will rather than formulating within your own minds that version of service which you imagine, which you think is ideal. My beloved souls, God’s plan is perfect. His Will is the ideal, and you, my beloved souls, must seek out this Truth, the Truth of God’s Will.

And this is challenging for many of you, for you still have much of your life and being immersed in the conditions of mankind, and the expectations which you carry are the results of these conditions and when we say you must be in the world but not of the world, this is our meaning, to not be in the conditions of mankind but to be in the Light of God’s Presence and reality, and each of you struggles with this. None of you are beyond it but you have the potential to do so for you have that great gift of Love in your soul shining forth, cleansing, strengthening, preparing and teaching you. And it is this great key, this great Love that will give you the strength to detach from these Earthly conditions, to rise above and to be fully in the flow of God’s Love and Will in the world.

When you are in it you feel the joy of it. But, unfortunately, this immersion is a temporary thing as you become distracted and are pulled at, partly by your own will and partly by the power of the human condition which compels you. And there is something within each one of you that fears the possibility of being fully enveloped in this reality of God’s Love. It is a reluctance to enter that pool of water, for you know the familiarity of the human condition and you feel the power of God’s Love condition and you are yet to decide which you prefer. This is almost universally true with each soul in this world. So few wish to be immersed in God’s great Love so fully. They feel that they will be vulnerable, that they will be hurt by others, that they will lose all that they have, that they will become lost in this Light.

Beloved souls, you have the angels of God supporting you. You have the power of God’s Love burning within you. You have a great desire within you to serve God. You have all that you require to be truly a part of this world and in so doing, what will you lose? What is to be lost is that which is not in harmony with this flow of Love and this you fear. You are still attached and longing to be loved by others, to be acknowledged, to have the material security that the world offers to be comfortable. And you hold on to your mind’s ideals and judgements, reality. And this, my beloved souls, is what you fear the most, to lose who you are and not knowing who you will become, to lose all that you have built over a lifetime and fearful that what you have labored in sweat and put great effort into to build your life will all disappear in a heartbeat in a choice to be with God fully.

My friends, it is not like that at all. Everything that you have within your life at this time may change and be influenced and transformed by the Father’s Love but it will not crumble and fall away as you imagine, but all will be enhanced and made more beautiful and in more harmony with your choice to be in this great River of Love, to be in this River always.

Yes, things within you will fall away, old habits and fears, error and judgements. Things will fall away, but, oh what freedom you will feel when these elements that are not in harmony with Love are no longer truly a part of you. What a dance of Joy will your life be. What a powerful opportunity to serve God given this renewed, reborn you.

So I tell you, my beloved souls, each of you has a choice. Each of you has a purpose. Each of you has what you require to serve God in a powerful way, in a way that few are strong enough to acknowledge and accept, but you, my beloved souls, have been nurtured and taught; you have grown in this Love; you have been given all that you require, great gifts and blessings, messages of truth, the flow of God’s Love. So much you have received over the years and it is now time to say *“I am willing to release my pain. I am willing to stop those habits that cause me pain. I am willing to let go of my judgements. I am willing to love myself fully and I want to be your servant, dear God, each day to be your servant.”* And in this prayer given with all truthfulness and sincerity, God will grant you these gifts, will pour His Love within you and all that you wish to release, all that is meant to be released will be released and you will walk this path with great freedom and joy, and those who would inhibit you and wish to control you will no longer have that influence upon you. You will be a strong and beautiful channel of Love and that which influences you will be that of God and His angels and those around you who support you in this effort.

It is a leap of faith. It is difficult to let go. You are fearful, I know, but you are strong; you are wise; you have a powerful soul and you will do this in accordance to your desire and decision to do so. God respects your will. He awaits your decision and He loves you. Whatever you decide, you are always loved and the angels will never turn away from you. But God and the angels are hopeful that you will ascend that great staircase to greater service, to become a powerful channel and you will do so in humility and grace and Love.

Beloved souls, I acknowledge your efforts. Each of you have grown mightily and have tried with great effort to be in the Light, to live the truths of God’s Love and to release those things that burden you, and I acknowledge all of these efforts. You have worked hard. You desire to do more and there is where my words come. To do more you must be more in that great flow of Love. You must release more. You must make greater prayers. You must let go of your illusions and embrace God’s reality, and in so doing, oh the doors that will open for you and the possibilities that will be your gifts and fate and opportunities for service.

Beloved souls, your friend Alec is always with you, and I embrace each one of you, and I am truly grateful to be a servant of Love for you beautiful souls who are reaching, ever reaching for Light. God bless you, God bless you. Remember you are never alone and shall never be alone and are always loved. Alec is with you. God bless you.
