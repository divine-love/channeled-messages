This Light shall ever be with you. Carry this lantern wherever you go. Carry the lantern of Love and be His Instruments of Love and Peace and Truth. I shall lead you, beloved souls, I shall lead you as you walk upon this path in service, in love, in humility and grace. So I shall lead you and be with you, for I love you. I am Jesus and I love you. You are my brothers and sisters and I walk with you in this world. God bless you.
