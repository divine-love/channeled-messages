# Prism of the Soul

Messages exploring the nature, faculties, and gifts of the soul — its capacity for Divine Love, its unique expression, its senses, and the gifts that emerge as it is awakened and transformed.

|---|---|---|

He urges the group to redouble their prayers for Divine Love as the key to unlocking the magnificent abilities that lie dormant within each soul. |
| [Come to Know Your Purpose](../messages/2015/11/2015-11-30-af-augustine.md) | 2015‑11‑30 | Augustine urges each soul to ask God to reveal the unique purpose He placed within them, teaching that this discovery brings the deepest joy and a life that falls into harmony. He reassures those who feel fear about what that purpose might require, promising that walking in God's Will brings not devastation but peace, strength, and a love beyond what the mind can currently imagine. |
| [The Time Comes for You to Express These Gifts](../messages/2015/12/2015-12-28-af-keea-atta-kem.md) | 2015‑12‑28 | Keea Atta Kem tells the group that the time has come to stop standing at the threshold and step fully into the flow of God's Love. She calls them to release judgment and division between themselves, to walk together in unity, and to open the mighty river of soul gifts within them to a world starving for spiritual nourishment. |
| [Your Beautiful Souls Are Eager](../messages/2016/01/2016-01-09-af-confucius.md) | 2016‑01‑09 | Confucius celebrates the community's eagerness and love for one another as they enter a new era of service, affirming that each soul carries unique and wondrous gifts waiting to be discovered and expressed in the world. He urges each one to act upon the guidance they receive from God, promising that as they do they will fulfill their purpose and become true instruments of Light and change. |
| [The Prism of Our Souls](../messages/2016/04/2016-04-04-af-keea-atta-kem.md) | 2016‑04‑04 | Keea atta Kem teaches that Divine Love acts as a prism through which the light of the soul is refracted into many unique gifts and colors, and that each soul possesses a distinct combination of these gifts — healing, perception, empathy, wisdom, communication — which God enlivens and orchestrates in beautiful and wondrous ways. |
| [How Divine Love Changes the Soul](../messages/2016/05/2016-05-16-af-keea-atta-kem.md) | 2016‑05‑16 | Keea Atta Kem describes the sequence by which Divine Love changes a soul: wounds heal, inherited and collected conditions fall away, and dormant gifts awaken as the encrustation shielding the soul dissolves. She explains that the soul then begins to influence the mind, opening perceptions the material mind cannot reach, and cautions that even these remain a reflection of the truth until the soul is fully redeemed. She closes with a prayer for the Divine Love. |
| [The Opening of Your Soul Perceptions](../messages/2016/10/2016-10-16-af-augustine.md) | 2016‑10‑16 | Augustine teaches the difference between the awareness of the mind and the awareness of the soul. The world of man is shadowy, he says, because the material mind is filled with the thoughts and ideas of others implanted through education and experience, so choices rest on perceptions with no basis in truth. The soul has its own mind and faculties, unlike those of the material mind, and Divine Love magnifies them exponentially. He describes how the Celestial spirits add to a limited human vision, and says the opening of these perceptions continues for all eternity, their own included. |
| [The Budding Potentials of Our Souls](../messages/2016/11/2016-11-29-af-care.md) | 2016‑11‑29 | Care joins the circle in its prayers and asks the group to let go of their expectations and allow God to orchestrate their work. God's plan for each of them and for all of them together opens like a rose, one petal at a time, and planning for God only diverts their efforts. Each of them is a unique rose who will flower, and if they could see with the eyes of the soul, their worries would fall away. |
| [Be Scientists of the Soul](../messages/2016/12/2016-12-05-af-keea-atta-kem.md) | 2016‑12‑05 | Keea Atta Kem tells the circle that its prayers for a questioning soul have tilled fertile ground, and that many of them will carry God's message of love to every continent. The work seems slow, but it is building momentum. The mind interprets truth through experience and education and holds only reflections and shadows of it, while the awakened soul understands truth as the mind cannot. Mankind has filled millions of books with facts and neglected the soul, so she calls them to be scientists of the soul, studying its development through prayer, contemplation and commitment. |
| [Know Your Awakened Soul](../messages/2016/12/2016-12-08-af-augustine.md) | 2016‑12‑08 | Augustine tells his students what an awakened soul brings. The first change is a greater sensitivity to the energetic conditions of a world gone astray. Then come soul perceptions that let them glimpse what is coming, work in close rapport with the angels, and see clearly what God's Will asks in each moment. They will need these perceptions, for the planet is depleted and upheavals will come, though God will give all a chance to rebuild in harmony. The journey is incremental, and he asks them to pray daily for God's wisdom and to trust in His care. |
| [Seek Truth Within Your Soul](../messages/2016/12/2016-12-28-af-alec-gaunt.md) | 2016‑12‑28 | Alec honours the circle's years of faithful gathering and reminds them that the Truth burning within them touches souls wherever they go, often without their knowing. With changes near, he asks them to stay awake to God's guidance, which comes to each according to their gifts, from a passing thought to detailed visions, and to test the truth with God rather than adopt the speculations of others. As they exercise the faculties of the soul and pray for the Love, the faculties grow stronger and clearer, and they become a rock in the storms to come. |
| [Accept Every Unique Soul: Their Differences Will Not Harm You Spiritually](../messages/2017/01/2017-01-16-af-keea-atta-kem.md) | 2017‑01‑16 | Keea atta Kem addresses the anxiety of spiritually sensitive souls who feel affected by the world's conditions, teaching that increased sensitivity is a natural sign of soul growth, that thoughts attract like energies by law, and that the Love within a growing soul provides powerful protection — so there is no need for fear, only joyful acceptance of each unique soul walking their own path toward God. |
| [God's Love Awakens the Gifts of the Soul](../messages/2018/01/2018-01-29-af-john-the-beloved.md) | 2018‑01‑29 | John the Beloved teaches that within each soul God has planted a unique garden of gifts and potentials awaiting germination through the Light of Divine Love. He urges each soul to look within, honour its own unique beauty, and express its gifts freely rather than copying another, for as each soul's lantern is lit, it will draw and awaken others until the whole world is alight. |
| [Awakening of Your Gifts Requires God's Love, Faith and Timing](../messages/2018/07/2018-07-13-af-keea-atta-kem.md) | 2018‑07‑13 | Keea atta Kem teaches that as souls gather and build light together, God is able to use this collective energy to bring blessings to those connected to them - including souls who long without knowing it. She cautions against the mind's eagerness and ambition interfering with soul gift development, teaching that gifts must unfold in harmony with God's timing and Will, and that the feeling of harmony within is the reliable guide for knowing when one is in the right flow. |
| [Does Every Soul Have a Purpose?](../messages/2019/01/2019-01-29-af-augustine.md) | 2019‑01‑29 | Augustine responds to a question about whether every soul has a purpose, teaching that God has imbued each soul with gifts, abilities, and an invitation to fulfil a unique role in His plan. He assures those gathered that their purpose is already within them, drawing them forward naturally like gravity, and that God has arranged every experience of their lives to prepare them for its full expression in Love. |
| [Faculties of the Soul](../messages/2019/02/2019-02-01-af-josephus.md) | 2019‑02‑01 | Josephus, a philosopher and teacher of the Jewish faith now in the Celestial Kingdom, delivers a structured teaching on the faculties of the soul, distinct from the material mind and held in chambers that God's Essence opens. He teaches that these faculties are unique to each soul, must be experienced rather than understood by the mind, and are refined and transformed when ignited by Divine Love. |
| [Walking God's Path of Divine Love Will Bring You Upon A Different Road](../messages/2019/04/2019-04-07-af-augustine.md) | 2019‑04‑07 | Augustine teaches that the power of God's Love within human souls is what enables the Celestial Angels to reach and speak to mortals, and that every member of the circle carries a myriad of hidden gifts the material mind blocks from awareness through its fear, its demand for control, and its pressure to walk as others walk. He calls those gathered to step forward in faith without full understanding, releasing the shackle that says worthiness requires perfection, and promises that as the soul awakens these gifts become self-evident and will be used by God in surprising ways. |
