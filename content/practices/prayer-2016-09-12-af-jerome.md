Heavenly Father, these are my burdens, this is my cloak of pain, this is the armor which I don in order to navigate this life and I wish that you would take it all, wipe me clean, dear Father. Make me pure with your Love. Allow me to walk in joy, in strength, in Love, in clarity, in Light.
