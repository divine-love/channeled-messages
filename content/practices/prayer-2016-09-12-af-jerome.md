## The prayer

Heavenly Father, these are my burdens, this is my cloak of pain, this is the armor which I don in order to navigate this life and I wish that you would take it all, wipe me clean, dear Father. Make me pure with your Love. Allow me to walk in joy, in strength, in Love, in clarity, in Light.

## How it is given

Jerome gives the prayer to a seeker he has just told to look honestly at what
lies within: "It is for you to be strong enough to look. It is for you to have
enough faith to lay these burdens to God and say..." He follows it with what it
does: "With this prayer you will find your way and there will be a great
healing in your being and a joy will grow where there was once tears, and a
perception and a shift of perspective will emerge within you bringing forth
greater clarity within your mind and an awakening of the mind of your soul."

Read in its place in the full message: [2016-09-12-af-jerome](../messages/2016/09/2016-09-12-af-jerome.md)

## Text fidelity

In the message the prayer is embedded in Jerome's speech, italicised and inside
quotation marks: `*"Heavenly Father, these are my burdens..."*`. Standing alone
here, the enclosing italic markers and quotation marks are dropped and every
word and mark inside them is preserved exactly as published. "armor" is the
spelling in the source and is not normalised to Canadian spelling, since the
message text is not altered.
