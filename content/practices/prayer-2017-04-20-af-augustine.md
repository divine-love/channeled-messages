I lay down my will for your Will. I give up those constructs of the mind, ideas and dreams that I have concocted to give me a sense of security and I now embrace the Truth, I now embrace the reality of your beautiful Creation, of your ultimate and wise Will for me, the destiny that I am to walk as a child of God.
