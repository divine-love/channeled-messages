### 2017-09-12-af-augustine

Goes after the `2017-08-21-af-matthew` entry and before `### 2017-09-13-af-keea-atta-kem`. The three 2017-09-12 entries go together in this order: augustine, confucius, martin-luther.

```
### 2017-09-12-af-augustine | Release of Limitations | 2017-09-12
- `fear-of-full-immersion` **[Elaboration]** : the fear named as the reason a seeker keeps its limits, and the mind named as what it fears to lose. The limits are held out of fear, "It is only your need to set limits because you are afraid that if you released these limitations you will lose something vital," and the Foundation's answer is given again, "But I tell you, my beloved souls, you will not lose anything. You will gain much in your willingness to let go and allow God to work His miracles within you." These conditions will become purified." Read with 2017-01-23-af-alec-gaunt, which takes the identity step through the outward self; this message takes it through the mind.
- NOTE (witness, `soul-expiation`): the change made from within, "as Divine Love incrementally builds within you and changes these conditions from the inside outward," with the painful sight the Love gives along the way, "at times you feel shocked, in despair when you see how you do not love yourselves."
- NOTE (witness, `self-love-foundation`): self-love deepening as the old conditions are shed, "you begin to love yourselves with greater depth," with what must go named, "self-judgment, self-recrimination."
- NOTE (witness, `mind-vs-soul`): the mind changed by the Love rather than lost, "It impinges upon your psyche, your mind, your thinking. It gives a different perspective, one that is not so complex, but simple and pure," and "Simplicity will reign within your consciousness. Yet, the power and wisdom of this will bring you great knowledge and perception that comes from the soul." Considered as a Chrysalis and set aside: it says what the mind will become rather than describing the state as it stands.
- NOTE (witness, `incremental-journey`): the change by increments, "Divine Love incrementally builds within you," with the seeing only begun, "It does not stop there, this is only the beginning."
```

### 2017-09-12-af-martin-luther

Goes directly after the 2017-09-12-af-confucius entry, and before `### 2017-09-13-af-keea-atta-kem`.

```
### 2017-09-12-af-martin-luther | Understanding the Laws of Love and the Regeneration of Our Souls | 2017-09-12
- `two-paths` **[Elaboration]** : the Divine road's way of purifying stated as a Law above the others. "There is one Law that supersedes all others and that is the Law of Divine Love," and with it the soul is "able to sidestep many of these conditions that God has laid before each soul in order for it to be purified and come to a place, a point of pristine condition of natural love." The cleansing the natural road works by effort is done by the Love itself, "When the Divine Love enters the soul in sufficient quantity, there is no need for these efforts to purify the condition of the soul through the effects of natural love. For this purification is enacted in a spontaneous way through the influence of the Love within each soul." Effort still helps on the Divine road, "Yes, this process may be sped up by your conscious efforts to put aside any conditions that are negative and not in harmony with this Love." Read with 2016-05-02-af-mary, which has the Love over-riding the Law of Compensation.
- NOTE (witness, `incremental-journey`): the seeker between the two, "they often have one foot upon the Divine Path and one foot in the human condition," and the forward movement under the wavering, "but the progress continues forward and you become a little stronger with each day."
- NOTE (witness, `free-will-and-self-responsibility`): the reason the spirits will not do the work for the seeker, "The angels cannot spoon feed you forever. This would not facilitate a strengthening of your being," with the responsibility placed, "Many turn away because they do not understand their responsibility in the regeneration of the soul." It repeats the reason 2015-03-29-af-seretta-kem gives without developing it.
- NOTE (sighting, `pentecost`): a swift way to at-onement set against the common gradual one, "This can be a powerful and swift way of bringing a soul to at-onement with God. But this is not a common experience, for most souls come to God in a very gradual way." The swift way here is a conscious choice for Love and harmony, not a sudden infilling.
- NOTE (sighting, `god-or-the-human-condition`): the choice put between the power of Love and the human condition, "At times the conditions of this world pull you away because you have chosen to walk in this condition rather than to choose the power of Love." Not the minting case, since the pair is Love and the human condition rather than God's Will and the human condition.
- NOTE (context): Martin Luther answers the circle's conversation, "I have been listening to your conversation this evening," and states that the outpouring of the Love will one day end, "even that, as you have read, there will come a time when that possibility has ended."
```

### 2017-12-12-af-seretta-kem

Goes at the end of the file, after the `2017-11-03-af-nikola-tesla` entry (its last line ends "You are causing quite a stir in spirit.").

```
### 2017-12-12-af-seretta-kem | On Soul Expiation | 2017-12-12
- `soul-expiation` **[Elaboration]** : the mechanism from the encrustations to the momentum that clears them. The encrustations never seal the soul completely, "There are always cracks and fissures upon the surface of the soul allowing in this Light so that it is not a complete sealing off of the soul from God," and expiation is defined, "simply the release of these conditions through a number of means including the physical," opening "passages for the Father's Love to more readily enter into the soul." Then the turn by which the clearing gathers speed, "And with the reinforcing condition of Love and joy comes more Love and joy. The momentum builds and all conditions around the soul, all error of the mind begins to disintegrate."
- `soul-expiation` **[Objection-removed]** : clears the belief that every encrustation must be released before the Love can be received. "The error which teaches that every encrustation must be released in order to receive this gift, is not true," because God finds a way in, "There is always a way, there is always some route to the soul, which God, through His Holy Spirit, will find a way." Seeing one's own patterns helps and is not required, "The recognition of your patterns, of those conditions that block you from God are powerful and very helpful indeed and bring about swift results in your soul's awakening and growing within the Love. But they are not mandatory, for these conditions may fall away very slowly and subtly with the inflowing of the Divine Love."
- NOTE (build, `soul-expiation`): this message and 2023-02-10-af-augustine clear the same objection, and this one gives the route by which the Love enters. Open question for build: which of the two anchors the section.
- `divine-love-without-knowing` **[Elaboration]** : why the Love can enter unfelt. The mind's fixed structures keep it from registering the inflow, "Their minds are set in a certain way, certain conditions and attitudes and structures within that are set and not flexible. So that when the blessing of Divine Love flows into the soul, the mind finds it extremely difficult to be aware of this condition and experience," the soul's own blockages joining in "a sort of numbness," and yet the Love comes in, "At times the mind does not recognize this experience and yet this experience is real and happens despite this lack of sensitivity and awareness." It gives the cause of the not-feeling that 2019-02-05-af-keea-atta-kem states.
- NOTE (witness, `armour-of-the-world`): the armour as what seals the soul from God, with the crack as God's way in, "these encrustations are like an armor protecting the soul and sealing off the soul from God. Yet God may find a way through this seemingly impenetrable condition of encrustations," as at 2017-01-30-af-augustine.
- NOTE (witness, `free-will-and-self-responsibility`): the release from other people's choices, applied to sharing the Truth, "It is not your responsibility to ensure that they follow one route or another, merely that you give them the Truth of the Father's Love and demonstrate to them your own Light and understanding. They must choose as this is God's gift for every soul with the blessing of free will."
- NOTE (witness, `prayer-changes-world-conditions`): prayer aimed at the end the registry names, "God puts the desire for His Love within other souls and you must pray for this gift to come," for souls whose condition cannot be seen, "You can pray for each individual soul however and pray that they may recognize that desire within them and turn that desire in prayer to receive this gift of Love."
- NOTE (witness, `redemption-universal`): no soul out of reach, "though a soul may be drowning in the darkness of the conditions of this world, they too are capable of receiving this gift, for there is always a way for God to place His Love within their souls, provided that longing exists," and of the resistant, "do not think that they will never find their at-onement with God through receiving the Divine Love, for they will."
- NOTE (witness, `simplicity-of-the-path`): the ministry kept simple, "It is not so complicated my beloveds," and "So this is how you will move in the world my beloved brothers and sisters, in this simple way. Not with harsh expectations of how another's journey must be."
- NOTE (witness, `speaking-the-truth`): the Truth spoken as one's own experience, "it is your testimony, your acknowledgement of the power of this Love that you must bring forth to your brothers and sisters in this world."
- NOTE (considered and set aside, `divine-love-healing`): the healing here is the soul's expiation, which is `soul-expiation`'s ground. The message makes no comparison between Divine Love and other healing, and does not say the blessing cannot pass from one person to another.
```
